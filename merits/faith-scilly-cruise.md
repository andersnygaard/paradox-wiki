## Faith: a ~500-mile River Crouch-to-Scilly Isles cruise, in company with Little Jim (2008)

Bill Serjeant's "Faith" made a nearly two-month cruise from the River Crouch to the Scilly Isles and back to Plymouth, sailed initially solo and, for the Scillonian leg, in company with "Little Jim" — the pair crossing to Tresco, riding out a severe gale at anchor in New Grimsby, and towing each other's boats between Fowey and Plymouth once the outboard proved the more reliable engine of the two.[1] The two accounts of the distance differ: the day-by-day cruise log puts it at some 800 miles from the River Crouch, while the abridged version published in *Messing About In Boats* as "With Great Britain's Dinghy Cruisers: Faith's Cruise" estimates the whole passage at around 500 sea miles.[1][2] Full account at [boats/faith.md](/boats/faith.md#to-the-scilly-isles-and-back-with-little-jim); the magazine coverage is described at [references/messing-about-in-boats.md](/references/messing-about-in-boats.md#a-british-paradox-in-a-dca-cruise-account-july-2009).
