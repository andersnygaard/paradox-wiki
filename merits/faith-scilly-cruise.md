## Faith: a ~500-mile River Crouch-to-Scilly Isles cruise, in company with Little Jim (2008)

Bill Serjeant's "Faith" made a nearly two-month cruise from the River Crouch to the Scilly Isles and back to Plymouth, sailed initially solo and, for the Scillonian leg, in company with "Little Jim" — the pair crossing to Tresco, riding out a severe gale at anchor in New Grimsby, and towing each other's boats between Fowey and Plymouth once the outboard proved the more reliable engine of the two.[1] The two accounts of the distance differ: the day-by-day cruise log puts it at some 800 miles from the River Crouch, while the abridged version published in *Messing About In Boats* as "With Great Britain's Dinghy Cruisers: Faith's Cruise" estimates the whole passage at around 500 sea miles.[1][2] Full account at [boats/faith.md](/boats/faith.md#to-the-scilly-isles-and-back-with-little-jim); the magazine coverage is described at [references/messing-about-in-boats.md](/references/messing-about-in-boats.md#a-british-paradox-in-a-dca-cruise-account-july-2009).

## Attested from outside, as it happened

Every other account of this cruise is by one of the two people who made it. [intheboatshed.net](/references/intheboatshed.md), the weblog of UK boat-design writer Gavin Atkin, followed it from outside and published three notices while it was under way — the archive's only contemporaneous, non-participant record of the passage.[3][4][5]

Writing on **6 April 2008**, with Faith weatherbound at Ramsgate, Atkin set out the plan in advance: Bill was sailing from **Burnham on Crouch** along the South Coast to the West Country, "where he intends to meet and sail in company with another *Paradox* sailor Al Law."[3] That the rendezvous with "Little Jim" was the object of the cruise from the outset — not something arranged along the way — rests on this outside statement of intent as much as on the participants' own planning threads. He also relayed the early progress: an anchorage in the **Swale**, then out along the **North Kent Coast**, then a couple of days waiting out bad weather at **Ramsgate**.[3]

On **7 May 2008** he recorded the **outward** arrival at Plymouth — the leg the *Messing About In Boats* account, which describes the return to Plymouth at the cruise's end, does not date.[4] On **19 May 2008**, two days after the fact, he recorded the arrival at the Scilly Isles, "at the end of his epic cruise starting from the coast of Essex," made "in a **Matt Layden**-designed 14ft *Paradox*," and confirmed independently that Bill "was accompanied for the last leg by his friend Al Law, another British *Paradox* builder-owner."[5]

None of this is new information about the sailing; Atkin was reading [Bill's Log](/references/bills-log.md) and relaying it, and is [secondary](/paradox.md) on every detail he reports. Its value is that the three central claims of the merit — **that the Scillies were reached, that the cruise began in Essex, and that a second Paradox sailed the final leg in company** — are attested by a disinterested observer, contemporaneously, and not only by the two sailors involved.
