# The Texas 200, and a Port Townsend Wooden Boat Show showing

The Texas 200 coastal raid and a Port Townsend Wooden Boat Show appearance stand as real-world evidence of a Paradox's cruising ability.[1][2]

## The Texas 200, and a Port Townsend Wooden Boat Show showing

The **Texas 200** is a long-distance small-boat coastal raid along the Texas coast, used by owners as evidence of a boat's real-world cruising ability. The [Paradox](/paradox.md) that [Lezlie Henson](/people/lezlie-henson.md) built in Texas as "Tardis" — renamed **"Scout"** by Sean Mulligan and later owned by **[Chris Curtis](/people/chris-curtis.md)** of Sandpoint, Idaho — ran the Texas 200 in **2014**, under Mulligan — entered that year as a fundraiser for the **Livestrong Foundation**, her sides carrying the names of cancer survivors, those still in the fight, and those already lost[4] — covering the full **200 miles over five days** in conditions from light air to over 30 knots, and towing a fellow competitor's boat, "Crippled Duck," through 20–25 kt wind along the way.[2][5] The same boat has been **shown several times at the Port Townsend Wooden Boat Show** — "a great boat in almost every way."[1] Full record at [boats/scout.md](/boats/scout.md#heavy-wind-sailing-and-the-2014-texas-200), with Chris Curtis's ownership at [people/chris-curtis.md](/people/chris-curtis.md).

A different Paradox, [Michael Beebe](/people/michael-beebe.md)'s Rockport-built boat, ran the event two years earlier, in **2012**, filmed executing a particularly difficult tack under sail alone at the Padre Island Yacht Club in Corpus Christi. Full account at [people/michael-beebe.md](/people/michael-beebe.md#the-first-paradox-2012).[3]

**[Frederic Tallman](/people/frederic-tallman.md)** registered "[The Edge](/boats/edge.md)" for the event's **June 2020** running, inviting other owners to join him and citing Mulligan's and Beebe's earlier runs as precedent; full account at [boats/edge.md — a planned 2020 Texas 200 entry](/boats/edge.md#a-planned-2020-texas-200-entry).[6]
