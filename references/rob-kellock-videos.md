# Rob Kellock's YouTube channel

**Rob Kellock's YouTube channel** carries a run of owner-authored videos of his own Paradox, "[Mini](/boats/mini.md)," posted between **16 December 2017** and **29 August 2023**, a wider and earlier-starting span than the archive's Facebook-era record alone would suggest. The channel's own display name credits the uploader as "Rob Kellock." Twelve videos on it carry material not otherwise cited anywhere in the bundle, and several of the facts they carry, the October 2018 grounding aside, exist nowhere else in the archive: a Lake Ellesmere camping trip and a rig-tuning discovery, the deliberate GPS-tracked hull-speed test,[1] a ballast-pump refinement, and the wholly uncited 2021-2023 stretch of cruising, Tauranga Harbour and two trips to Queen Charlotte Sound.

## What the channel is worth as evidence

It is **owner-authored and contemporaneous**, each video posted close to the passage or trial it records, and it is one of the archive's fullest **visual** records of a single Paradox's handling across a range of conditions: light air, a 25-plus knot gale, a night crossing, a grounding recovery, and years of routine winter sailing on the same two home waters. Against the mailing list and Facebook record of the same builder it is largely complementary rather than duplicative: several videos accompany a written trip report already cited elsewhere, filling in footage of a passage the prose already tells in full; others, particularly from 2019 onward, are the only record of a cruise at all.

Its limits should be stated plainly. It is **one owner and one boat**, sailed to her own builder's own preferences throughout rather than to any standard. Several videos carry captions only in their written description rather than a spoken transcript, so content drawn from a description rather than narration is cited as such below. Two videos found during the sweep for this page belong to a different channel entirely, a sailing companion's own account of a shared passage, and are cited separately, not folded into this table.

## Citing this source

Each video resolves at `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<id>`, with the ids listed in the table above. Cite in the form:

> [Rob Kellock's YouTube channel](/references/rob-kellock-videos.md), Rob Kellock, "[title]," YYYY-MM-DD — [detail].

Where the material comes from a video's **written description** rather than its spoken narration, the citation says so. Content is cited in the past tense and dated to the video it comes from, consistent with the bundle's historical-archive convention.
