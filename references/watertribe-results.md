# WaterTribe Everglades Challenge results (watertribe.com)

**WaterTribe** (`watertribe.com`) organises a family of engine-free small-boat races down Florida's Gulf and Atlantic coasts, among them the roughly 300-mile **Everglades Challenge**, run every spring from Fort DeSoto to Key Largo by way of three checkpoints, and the roughly 1,200-mile **Ultimate Florida Challenge**, which adds a full circumnavigation of the state. **watertribe.com is the race's own publishing platform**, and its per-year results pages are the primary record of who entered, in what class and boat, and how they finished.

It is an **organisational record, not an authored account**: each results page lists every entrant by tribe name (and, in most years, a real name), class, and a split time at each checkpoint or a DNF/DNS, published by the race itself rather than reconstructed from outside reports. For this bundle it establishes the wider Everglades Challenge record of **Karank**, the WaterTribe name of [John H. Wright](/people/john-h-wright.md), a correspondent on the Paradox builders mailing list who is not known to have sailed a Paradox himself.

## What the record establishes

Each year's page is the registrar's own ledger, not a narrative: a competitor entry gives tribe name, real name where the entrant supplied one, class, and either a split time at each checkpoint or a DNF ("did not finish") from that point on, or DNS ("did not start"). Read year by year, Karank's own results confirm a run of six Everglades Challenge entries: DNF at every checkpoint in **2010** and **2011**; a **finish in 2012**, crewed as "Lugnut and Karank" (Gary Blankenship and John Wright), Class 4 Double Male, in 5 days 17 hours 31 minutes; a DNF at every checkpoint again in **2014**; and a DNF after the first checkpoint in **2015** (reached in 0 days 14 hours 58 minutes) and **2016** (reached in 1 day 1 hour 52 minutes). Every entry runs under **Class 4 (Monohull Sailboats)**; no entry records a boat name.[1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Limits

The record is **thin by design**. A results row is name, class, and split times or a DNF/DNS marker; it gives no reason for a withdrawal and no boat description beyond the class, and in two of the six years (2010 and 2014) the "aka" field reads simply "Anonymous" rather than a real name, a gap the results pages themselves never explain.

## What it adds to the bundle

- **A six-year racing career told in outline**, spanning 2010 to 2016 with one finish, correcting a single-DNF understatement drawn from the mailing list alone.
- **Gary Blankenship's full name**, attached to the tribe name "Lugnut" carried on the mailing list without one.
- **The 2012 finish's own figures** — Class 4 Double Male, 300 miles, 5 days 17 hours 31 minutes — independent of, and consistent with, the mailing list's own account of the same race.

The person is drawn out at [John H. Wright](/people/john-h-wright.md).

## Citing this source

Each year's results resolve at a fixed address, `https://watertribe.com/Events/ChallengeResults.aspx?RaceID=<event><year>` — for example `RaceID=EC2012` for the 2012 Everglades Challenge. Cite in the form:

> [WaterTribe Everglades Challenge results](/references/watertribe-results.md) — EC[year] results — [detail].

As a dated snapshot of a results table, its content is cited in the past tense and dated to the record it comes from, consistent with the bundle's historical-archive convention.
