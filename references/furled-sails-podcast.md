# Furled Sails podcast

**Furled Sails** (`furledsails.com`) is a weekly sailing interview podcast — billed on air as "the world's first sailing podcast" — hosted by **Noel and Christy Davis**, who put a different sailor in front of the microphone each week and closed every show reading listener mail. Its relevance here is direct rather than incidental: the show ran a **two-part interview with [David and Mindy Bolduc](/people/bolduc.md)** in July 2006, and Noel Davis was himself a member of the [Paradox builders mailing list](/references/mailing-list.md), posting there under his own name over a "FurledSails Sailing Podcast" signature — which is how the show's Layden-relevant episodes reached the builders' community in the first place.[1][2]

## The episodes drawn on here

- **#45, "Microcruising" (2006-07-09)** — the first half of the interview: the Layden design family and how it evolved, [Little Cruiser](/matt-layden/little-cruiser.md) as a cruising boat, the 1994 joint Bahamas cruise with Matt Layden, the economics of a fifteen-footer, and prudence and weather doctrine.[3]
- **#46, "Microcruising part 2" (2006-07-16)** — the second half: sculling and the yuloh, outboards and fuel, the WaterTribe challenges, entering inlets at night by ear, and the case for a small boat as a "disposable" cruiser.[4]

Two further *Furled Sails* interviews are named in the archive without being drawn on directly: **#73**, with WaterTribe founder Steve Isaac, in which Matt Layden's collaboration on Isaac's own Tridarka Raider is disclosed (see [Matt Layden — racing beyond Paradox](/matt-layden/biography.md#racing-beyond-paradox)), and an interview with **Sven Yrvind** (see [Sven Yrvind](/people/yrvind.md)).[1][5]

## Its evidentiary value and limits

Its value is that it is **spoken, dated, first-person testimony from the archive's deepest firsthand authorities on Matt Layden**, given in a form none of them ever wrote down. The Bolducs' written record — the [microcruising.com](/references/microcruising-website.md) articles and their [mailing-list](/references/mailing-list.md) posts — is organised around boats and techniques; an unscripted interview is not, and it produced material that appears nowhere else: **Matt Layden's colorblindness** and what it cost the Bolducs one night mid-Gulf-Stream, his ability to **scull eight to ten hours** at a stretch, his **sailing Enigma home** from a race rather than accepting a trailer, and a **late-season hurricane he rode out aboard Little Cruiser** in the Exumas. It also sits usefully in time, in mid-2006, between the 2001–2005 mailing-list traffic and the later microcruising.com articles, and so gives independently dated corroboration of several figures the written sources state only once.

Its limits follow from its form. It is an **unrehearsed conversation between four people** — two guests and two hosts — so dates, counts, and names are given from memory and are sometimes hedged aloud ("since, I think, 1991") or restated inconsistently within a single episode. Attribution between the two guests is not always recoverable from the audio, since Dave and Mindy finish each other's sentences throughout; a statement is therefore attributed here to **the Bolducs jointly** unless the recording makes the speaker plain. Nothing in the episodes was published as text, and proper nouns spoken once in passing are the least reliable part of the record. The standing rule applied throughout: where the podcast and a written source disagree on a number or a date, the **written source is followed and the difference recorded**, and a name heard only on the air is used only where an independent source in the archive already carries it.

Three such reconciliations are worth stating outright, because each is cited in the pages that draw on this source. Matt Layden's **Ultimate Florida Challenge** run is placed "last year" in the July 2006 recording, but it was sailed in **March 2006**, four months earlier — loose speech, not a second race. The couple date their acquisition of Little Cruiser to "**I think, 1991**," against the **1992** their own website gives; the website is followed. And **Swamp Thing**'s build year is given on air as **1986** — "two years earlier than our own boat," and Little Cruiser is a 1988 boat — where microcruising.com says **1985**; this bundle still follows the website, but the podcast means the 1986 date comes from Dave Bolduc himself and not only from the later third-party account that also gives it (see [Swamp Thing](/matt-layden/swamp-thing.md)).

## Provenance and dating

Both episodes were **released weekly in July 2006** and are dated on air in their opening seconds. They are conversational audio, recorded remotely with the guests at home in North Carolina — Mindy Bolduc says at the close of the second episode that she is already in bed after a full shift — and each runs a little under an hour. The show's own back catalogue was published as numbered articles with show notes on `furledsails.com`, and it is those numbered show-note pages that the builders' list linked to when pointing members at these interviews.[1]

An outside notice corroborates the back catalogue from a second direction. In **October 2008** the American small-craft weblog [70.8%](/references/70point8-blog.md) recommended the show to its own readers as "an invaluable resource" and listed among its subjects "Serge Testa, Webb Chiles, **Sven Yrvind, Dave and Mindy Balduc** [sic]" — independent confirmation that both the Bolduc interview and the [Yrvind](/people/yrvind.md) interview were live on the site at that date, the latter otherwise attested here only by a mailing-list post. The same notice is the archive's only outside description of the programme itself, crediting it to Noel Davis, "who shares his microphone with Kristy" — the co-host's name spelled Christy on the show's own pages.[6]

## Citing this source

Episodes are cited by number, title, and release date, with the position in the recording as the locator:

> [Furled Sails podcast](/references/furled-sails-podcast.md) #45, "Microcruising" (Noel & Christy Davis, 2006-07-09, at 2:30) — [detail].

Each episode has a show-notes page at `http://www.furledsails.com/article.php3?article=<id>`, where the id is the site's own article number and **not** the episode number. Three mappings are attested in the archive: episode **#45 → `article=674`**, episode **#46 → `article=675`**, and episode **#73 → `article=702`**.[1] No general formula for converting an episode number to an article id is on record, so an episode not listed above is located by its number, title, and date rather than by a reconstructed address.
