# Saanich News (Vancouver Island local press)

**Saanich News** (`saanichnews.com`) is a Black Press Media community newspaper serving Saanich and the wider south Vancouver Island, sharing regional copy across the group's island titles. It carries one article of interest here: a **5 June 2026** feature by **Kari Fredheim** on the island teams entered in that year's [Race to Alaska](/merits/race-to-alaska.md), the only conventional press coverage of the [Paradox](/paradox.md) entry, Team Forget Me Knot.[1]

It is **incidental coverage, not a profile**. The article is about two Campbell River teams; the Paradox appears in its opening paragraphs as the third island entry and in its closing line as an Instagram account to follow, and is never described, photographed, or quoted.[1] Its value is precisely that thinness — it is an independent, dated, non-participant record that the entry was live and being prepared, from a source with no stake in the race.

## What it establishes

Three things, each of which the race's own record does not supply.

It **timestamps intent**. Nine days before the start the article has Team Forget me Knot "in their last week of preparation," which is what makes the official **DNS** in [R2AK's own results](/references/r2ak-website.md) a late collapse rather than a never-started paper entry.[1] It **places the sailor** — "Forget me Knot is from the Comox Valley" — corroborating the Comox hometown on the race's registration from a second, local source.[1] And it **describes the course and the field** in plain terms: two stages, a 40-mile qualifying leg across the Strait of Juan de Fuca from Port Townsend to Victoria, then the run north with no prescribed course beyond two mandatory checkpoints; a start on 14 June 2026; and "around 75 boats registered … the largest in the race's history."[1]

Its limits are the obvious ones. It is **secondary** on everything to do with the boat — it names neither the design, the boat, nor the earlier attempt — and its authority is local-newsroom rather than specialist. Where it and the race's own record differ on a number, the race's record governs, per the bundle's [source-priority order](/paradox.md).
