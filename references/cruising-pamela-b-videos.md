# Cruising Pamela B (Mark Brothers's YouTube channel)

**Cruising Pamela B** (`youtube.com/@Cruising_Pamela_B`) is the YouTube channel on which **[Mark Brothers](/people/mark-brothers.md)** records cruising his [Paradox](/paradox.md) **"Pamela B"** — the boat built in South Australia by **[Nick Bell](/people/nickolas-bell.md)** as "**[Thor](/boats/thor.md)**" — on the coast, rivers, and lakes of **New South Wales, Australia**. Twenty-two videos are captured here, running from **30 December 2022** to **6 November 2024**.

Its single most important contribution to the archive is one sentence of channel description, which **closes the boat's ownership chain**: "Pamela B was built by Nick Bell in South Australia and sailed there as 'Thor' for several years before I was lucky enough to acquire her and fit her out for luxury dinghy sailing."[1] Nothing in the mailing-list record, which follows "Thor" from her 2014 build to 2018, and nothing in the Facebook record, which follows Brothers from his 2022 purchase onward, states the link — the two halves of the hull's life are joined only here.

## What the channel is worth as evidence

It is **owner-authored, contemporaneous, and continuous**: an eight-hundred-day record of one boat kept by the person sailing her, with passages posted within days of being made. Against the [Paradox Sailboat Facebook group](/references/paradox-facebook-group.md) record of the same owner it is **complementary rather than duplicative** — most of the videos are announced in the group as short posts, so the group carries the summary and the discussion it drew, while the channel carries the passage itself. Where a group post gives a distance and a top speed, the video generally shows the water it was made in.

It is also the archive's **fullest visual record of a Paradox fitted out for comfort**, a fit-out Brothers describes in the channel's own summary: 110 W of solar, a lithium iron battery, an electric outboard with solar recharging, lights, a fridge, a gimballed stove, satnav, VHF, radio and Bluetooth speakers, a dehumidifier, a fan, a hot shower, and a toilet — all inside a self-righting hull 13 ft 10 in (4.216 m) long, sailed entirely from inside.[1]

Its limits should be stated plainly. This is **one owner and one boat**, and a boat modified well away from stock: the [Mk3 sail](/components/rig/mk3-sail.md), a vang, halved water ballast, and an electric outboard used deliberately under sail all postdate the purchase, so the channel documents a **developed** Paradox rather than the design as drawn. Nine of the twenty-two videos carry **no caption track** at capture, including the channel trailer that supplies the provenance sentence above — that sentence comes from the video's **written description**, not from speech. Where transcripts do exist they are auto-generated, so punctuation and proper nouns are approximate and wording is cited as paraphrase unless clearly verbatim. Two further videos are 360-degree recordings whose value is entirely visual and not captured by transcript at all.

## Relationship to the rest of the record

Read alongside the bundle's other sources, this channel is the **hinge between two records that had never been joined**:

- **Before.** The [Paradox builders mailing list](/references/mailing-list.md) carries "Thor" from a birdsmouth mast in January 2015 through a June 2015 launch on the Murray River to an August 2018 regatta and capsize test — after which the boat leaves the record entirely. Bell's own later account says only that he sold her "after years of adventure," naming neither buyer nor destination.
- **After.** The [Paradox Sailboat Facebook group](/references/paradox-facebook-group.md) carries Brothers from February 2022, when he collected a seven-year-old secondhand Paradox from a South Australian seller at the New South Wales border, through the refit, the Mk3 sail, and years of NSW cruising — without ever naming the boat or her builder.

The channel supplies both missing halves at once: the boat's name, and the fact that the seller was her builder. The seven-year age Brothers gave in February 2022 agrees with a June 2015 launch, and the South Australian location agrees with where "Thor" was built and sailed.

The channel also **names the boat behind the [Mk3 sail](/components/rig/mk3-sail.md)**: Really Simple Sails' own account of the sail identifies its customer only as "Mark," but the video embedded in that page is titled "New Sail for Paradox Pamela B" and is hosted here.
