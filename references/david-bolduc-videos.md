# David Bolduc's YouTube videos

**[Dave Bolduc](/people/bolduc/dave.md)** posted **fourteen videos** of **[Little Cruiser](/matt-layden/little-cruiser.md)** and **[Enigma](/matt-layden/enigma.md)** under sail, across two channel handles and two upload waves. Nine were posted first, mostly filmed on an Outer Banks cruise and on the couple's home water, Lake Townsend near Greensboro; five more followed later, re-posted and extended, including his solo, engine-free Bahamas crossing aboard Enigma. None of the fourteen had been cited anywhere in the bundle before this page.

## What the videos are worth as evidence

They are **owner-filmed and largely unscripted**: a camera left running while sculling out of an anchorage, narrating a passage underway, or demonstrating a piece of gear to whoever might be watching. Several 2008 videos were re-uploaded in near-identical form in 2013, apparently from the same footage; where a fact appears in both, this bundle cites the fuller of the two and treats the shorter as a duplicate capture, not a second event.

Their chief value is **showing, not just telling**: in a two-part clip, Dave narrates the plan (he would handle the halyard, she would furl)[1] before [Mindy Bolduc](/people/bolduc/mindy.md) is seen operating Enigma's roller furling gear herself, unprompted, on their local Lake Townsend;[2] the yuloh's steering stroke (which side to push or pull to turn the boat) is demonstrated and narrated in a way the mailing-list record never quite spells out;[3] and the 2013 Bahamas crossing is narrated anchorage by anchorage, filling in a route the mailing-list and blog record leaves as a verdict with no geography.[4]

Their limits: the source is an **auto-generated caption track**, not a human transcript, so proper nouns are approximate; island names in particular are given phonetically and are reconciled against known Exuma cay names where the reconciliation is reasonably certain. Two of the fourteen videos carry no caption track at all, and one further video (`OM4ifw7rXCU`) is a music-only cruising montage with no spoken content. Dates are the videos' **publication** dates, not necessarily the date the footage was shot; several 2013 uploads plainly show 2006 material (matching Little Cruiser's Outer Banks cruise) or re-narrate a 2008 recording.

## Citing this source

Each video resolves at `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<id>`, with the ids listed in the table above. Cite in the form:

> [David Bolduc's YouTube videos](/references/david-bolduc-videos.md) (YouTube), Dave Bolduc, video id `<id>`, YYYY-MM-DD: [detail].

Content is cited in the past tense and dated to the video's publication date, consistent with the bundle's historical-archive convention. Where a 2013 upload duplicates a 2008 one, the citation notes the duplication rather than treating the two as independent sightings.
