# References

External sources and resources referenced by the archive (designer materials,
websites, plans, articles) treated as first-class concepts for citation.
Concepts are added here as they are sourced from the archive.

# Concepts

* [Paradox builders mailing list](mailing-list.md) - The Paradox community's mailing list, running continuously from Yahoo Groups (founded 2001) to groups.io's paradoxsailboat (from 2019), and the source archive for this bundle.
* [microcruising.com (David & Mindy Bolduc's website)](microcruising-website.md) - An authored website tied to the authorized Paradox plans distributor and owners of the Layden-designed Little Cruiser, documenting Matt Layden's whole family of boats, Bahamas/Outer Banks cruising, and detailed build/refit, sailmaking, and outfitting how-tos.
* [Messing About In Boats (MAIB) magazine](messing-about-in-boats.md) - The American small-boat magazine, edited by Bob Hicks, that is Paradox's original print venue — where Matt Layden's boats were first documented (from 1991), where the design and its plans-ordering information debuted (1995), and where Don Elliott's sixteen-installment "Building Paradox" construction series ran (1996–1997), followed by decades of sea-trial reports, sightings, and reader builds.
* [Dave's Wiki (aiki.pbworks.com — Dave Raftery)](daves-wiki.md) - A PBworks wiki compiling technical detail on Matt Layden's Paradox and Enigma micro-cruisers, including a Paradox/Enigma-360/Enigma-460 comparison, construction notes, and quotes from Matt Layden and others.
* [yrvind.com (Sven Yrvind's website)](yrvind-website.md) - The personal website and blog of Swedish designer-voyager Sven Yrvind, an authored first-person primary source on the chine runner's mechanism, dating, and history, on Matt Layden's boats and their dimensions, and on extreme-small-boat voyaging and design philosophy.
* [Sven Yrvind's book (*KONSTRUKTOREN*) and its English translation](yrvind-book.md) - Yrvind's Swedish-language book, including its chapter on Matt Layden and the Paradox chine runners, and the paradoxbuilders community's chapter-by-chapter volunteer effort — led by Kjell Karlsson — to translate it into English.
* [Andrew Layden's BGSU website (physics.bgsu.edu/~layden)](andrew-layden-website.md) - The personal academic homepage of an astronomer at Bowling Green State University — Matt Layden's brother — whose "Fun Stuff" branch hosts first-person build logs and photographs of Matt's boats (the 2005 Enigma, the 2007 Sand Flea, and a 2005 "Rob Roy-oid" sailing canoe) plus a Layden family and genealogy page.
* ["Building the Paradox" articles (Don Elliott)](building-the-paradox-articles.md) - Don Elliott's paid "Building Paradox" construction articles — a sixteen-installment series originally published in Messing About In Boats magazine (1996–97) and later sold by Don directly as a ~50-page compilation; this page covers the articles as an obtainable product (their construction content is captured at building-paradox-series.md).
* [The "Building Paradox" MAIB series — construction content](building-paradox-series.md) - The actual part-by-part construction content of Don Elliott's sixteen-installment "Building Paradox" series in Messing About In Boats (September 1996 – May 1997) — the published design particulars, the whole build sequence from bulkheads to rig, the WEST SYSTEM engineer's public critique of its epoxy claims, and the reader Q&A in which Dave Bolduc validates the chine runners.
* ["Beach Cruising and Coastal Camping" (Ida Little & Michael Walsh)](beachcruising-book.md) - A small-boat cruising book by Ida Little and Michael Walsh that discusses Matt Layden's micro-cruisers and includes a photograph of Swamp Thing (Matt aboard in the Abacos, p. 197).
* [Paradox Study Plans](study-plans.md) - Introductory study plans for the Paradox, distributed via the mailing list's files area.
* [Paradox building plans (source and cost)](building-plans.md) - Where to obtain the full Paradox building plans and what they cost, why the design resists simple scaling up, and answers to the interpretation questions that recur once a builder has the plans in hand.
* [Duckworks Magazine](duckworks-magazine.md) - An online small-boat / home-boatbuilding magazine that reprinted Paradox builders' tips.
* [Micro Cruising (Yahoo group)](micro-cruising-group.md) - A sibling Yahoo group for broader micro-cruising discussion beyond the Paradox design, founded around September 2001.
* ["Wind Vane Self-Steering" (book)](/operations/self-steering.md#a-purpose-built-option-wind-vane-self-steering) - A book containing simple, cheap plywood wind-vane plans suited to the Paradox — out of print as of December 2001, but purchasable through armchairsailor.com for $36.95; folded into [operations/self-steering.md](/operations/self-steering.md#a-purpose-built-option-wind-vane-self-steering).
* [Dave Carnell's boat-building articles](dave-carnell-articles.md) - An external boat-building website (home.att.net/~DaveCarnell) with articles on paint choice and treating wood rot/fungus for small plywood boats.
* [Materials sourcing links (epoxy, fiberglass, general boatbuilding, West System)](materials-sourcing-links.md) - Consolidated external materials-sourcing and reference links for Paradox builders — an epoxy/fiberglass supplier and the Kevlar-vs-fiberglass cost debate, general (non-Paradox-specific) boatbuilding community resources ([general boatbuilding resource links](materials-sourcing-links.md#general-boatbuilding-resource-links-cedar-strip-site-newsgroup-forum)), and West System Epoxy's moisture-barrier and wood/epoxy-sharpie reference material ([West System Epoxy resources](materials-sourcing-links.md#west-system-epoxy-resources-moisture-barriers-and-a-sharpie-building-article)).
* [Bill's Log (bills-log.blogspot.com — Bill Serjeant)](bills-log.md) - A daily-kept personal blog running 2005–2015, carrying a complete first-person Paradox build log for "Faith," her 2007–08 cruises, a full second-hand purchase-refit-resale chapter for "Minnow" ex-"Enuf," and a decade of appraisals, cost breakdowns, and surveys of other builders' boats.
* [Builders and Owners of Paradox Sailboats (2014 census)](builders-and-owners-census-2014.md) - A 29-boat worldwide table of Paradox builders and owners, first compiled in 2006 and republished with updates in October 2014 — the archive's most complete self-reported Paradox census, and the direct successor to the earlier UK/worldwide registry.
* [Small Sailboats / Paradox-UK websites (Bill Serjeant)](paradox-uk-website.md) - A UK-focused website pairing — smallsailboats.co.uk and its dedicated Paradox-UK offshoot — documenting Paradox building and sailing from a British builder's perspective.
* [Little Jim website (Alastair Law)](little-jim-website.md) - Alastair Law's own site documenting the construction (August 2001 – April 2003) and cruising of "Little Jim," the first Paradox built in the UK — a photo-and-text primary source behind much of that boat's page.
* [AYRS meeting talk — Alastair on "Little Jim" (May 2020)](ayrs-meeting-video.md) - A recorded May 2020 online talk to the Amateur Yacht Research Society giving a full firsthand walk-through of the UK Paradox "Little Jim" — its design, construction and stability, and seventeen years of cruising it — a late, mature primary source corroborating and extending the 2001–2004 archive accounts.
* [Fireside Yarns "MAYDAY MAYDAY MAYDAY" — Roger Barnes on crossing Bideford Bar (with "Little Jim")](roger-barnes-mayday-video.md) - An undated "Fireside Yarns" video by Dinghy Cruising Association president Roger Barnes recounting a late-September DCA passage in Bideford Bay, sailed in company with Alastair Law's green Paradox "Little Jim" — a rare third-party eyewitness account of the boat crossing a breaking bar in extreme seas.
* ["Sailing The Paradox" — DCA Bulletin 193 (Alastair Law)](dca-sailing-the-paradox.md) - A signed two-page owner's appraisal of the Paradox published in the Dinghy Cruising Association Bulletin, setting out fifteen advantages and eleven disadvantages after three seasons sailing "Little Jim" — the archive's most compact published statement of the design's practical trade-offs, and one of three independent print attestations of the builder's surname.
* ["Paradox" — DCA Bulletin 193 (Duncan Gilchrist)](dca-paradox-gilchrist.md) - A three-page evaluation in the Dinghy Cruising Association Bulletin by a declared sceptic who sailed the UK Paradox "Little Jim" at Chew Valley Lake in October 2006 — the archive's only independent, non-owner sail trial of the design in print, and the source that dates Bulletin 193 to after October 2006.
* [Sven Yrvind video — Matt Layden's chine runners (July 2025)](yrvind-chine-runner-video.md) - A July 2025 talk-to-camera video recounting a first encounter with Paradox in 1997, a visit to Matt Layden in Florida, how the chine runner works, and a 2011 chine-runner Atlantic crossing — a late retrospective first-person primary source corroborating and enriching the website account of the Yrvind–Layden friendship.
* [The small-boat library behind the Paradox](small-boat-library.md) - A working bibliography of the books, magazines, and manufacturer literature drawn on across Don Elliott's sixteen-part "Building Paradox" series, gathered by what each was used for.
* [Amateur Boat Builders' Association (ABBA) Newsletter (Western Australia)](abba-newsletter.md) - The newsletter of a Perth home-boatbuilding club, whose April/May 2021 issue carries an illustrated build-and-cruise report on the junk-rigged Paradox "Planktos" — the archive's only account of a completed, sailed junk-rig conversion, and the source of its most legible reproduction of Matt Layden's 1994 sail-plan drawing.
* [Dave Skaife's blog (daveskaife.wordpress.com)](dave-skaife-blog.md) - The WordPress cruising log of Dave Skaife, who built a Paradox in Hawaii and sailed it engine-free for four seasons (2014–2018) — Hawaii, the Gulf ICW, the Bahamas twice, and Cuba — the archive's longest continuous first-person Paradox cruising log after Bill's Log, plus a deliberate "comfortable microcruising" series of original outfitting and comfort essays.
* [Hogfish Design (Chris Morejohn's blog)](hogfish-design-website.md) - Chris Morejohn's own design blog (hogfishdesign.wordpress.com), where the Florida Keys sharpie builder in the Layden/Bolduc circle publishes boat-design concepts in his own words — including small, Paradox-inspired minimal cruisers alongside his larger liveaboard sharpies.
* [Eerik Mee's blog (paradoxbuild.blogspot.com)](eerik-mee-blog.md) - The Blogger build log of Eerik Mee (byline "Eerik") documenting the 2015–2019 build and 2017 launch of "THU," a 20% enlarged, Chinese-junk-rigged Paradox — the archive's only first-person record of a built scale-up and of a purpose-designed cambered junk rig on the design.
* [Pete Martin's blog (paradoxbuild.webs.com)](pete-martin-blog.md) - The build blog of Pete Martin ("My Paradox 'Johanna'"), documenting the 2006–2011 construction and 11 June 2011 launch of "JoHanna," a UK Paradox — the authored first-person record that carries the build through to completion and the water, where the mailing-list account stops mid-build in 2008.
* [Lezlie's World (Lezlie Henson's blog)](lezlie-henson-blog.md) - The Blogger diary whose 2011–2013 TARDIS build series is the first-person primary source behind "Scout" (ex-"Tardis") — the amateur, first-time build of a stock Paradox in the Houston area, built to a high standard, sold almost finished and never sailed by its builder.
* [Full n By (Sean Mulligan's blog)](full-n-by-blog.md) - The Blogger blog of Sean Mulligan, HPCC founder, recording in the owner's own voice the completion, September 2013 launch, and first sailing season of the Paradox "Scout" (ex-"Tardis") on Lake Havasu, Arizona — the first-person sequel to Lezlie Henson's build blog, covering his changes to the stock boat, the yuloh, and single-handed fit-out.
* [Paradox Sailboat Facebook group](paradox-facebook-group.md) - The private "Paradox Sailboat" Facebook group, founded February 2019 by Tim McKinney — an 833-post, 2019–2026 record of the modern Paradox community and the archive's principal source for the builders, boats, and sailing of the post-mailing-list era.
* [Anders Nygaard's Miramis interview](anders-nygaard-interview.md) - A solicited 2026-07-23 first-person interview with Anders Nygaard, builder of the 10%-enlarged Norwegian Paradox "Miramis" — the archive's first source gathered by direct interview rather than from an existing record, and the fullest single account of the design rationale, scaling philosophy, and composite-heavy construction of a 10% scale-up in progress.
* [Furled Sails podcast](furled-sails-podcast.md) - A weekly sailing interview podcast hosted by Noel and Christy Davis, whose two-part July 2006 "Microcruising" interview with David and Mindy Bolduc is the archive's only spoken first-person source on Matt Layden — the source of his colorblindness, his eight-to-ten-hour sculling stints, and the hurricane he rode out aboard Little Cruiser.
* [Matthew E. Noonan's Paradox build log (Cornell)](matthew-noonan-build-log.md) - A Cornell mathematician's daily journal of his Paradox build's first week, 6–16 June 2006 — the archive's fullest account of the days before a builder's first list post, frozen at Day 7 and never continued.
* [Sailing-Pathfinder (Benjamin Bloss's YouTube build log)](sailing-pathfinder-videos.md) - The twelve-video YouTube build log of Benjamin Bloss's Paradox "Pathfinder," running November 2021 to May 2025 — a talk-to-camera record of an unfinished build in progress, and the archive's fullest account of the hull-assembly stage narrated as it happened, including the reasoning behind choices the builder's Facebook posts record only as conclusions.
* [Cruising Pamela B (Mark Brothers's YouTube channel)](cruising-pamela-b-videos.md) - The twenty-two-video YouTube channel on which Mark Brothers records cruising his Paradox "Pamela B" in New South Wales, 2022-2024 — the only source identifying that boat as Nick Bell's earlier "Thor," and the fullest visual record of a Paradox fitted out for comfort.
* [David Bolduc's YouTube videos](david-bolduc-videos.md) - Fourteen videos posted by Dave Bolduc under two channel handles, 2008 and 2013, showing Little Cruiser and Enigma under sail and under yuloh on the Outer Banks, Lake Townsend and the 2013 solo Bahamas crossing.
* [Rob Kellock's YouTube channel](rob-kellock-videos.md) - The YouTube channel on which Rob Kellock recorded twelve sailing, cruising and systems videos of his own Paradox, "Mini," from her second sea trial in 2017 to a winter sail in 2023.
* [Race to Alaska official race record (r2ak.com)](r2ak-website.md) - The Race to Alaska's own published record — team pages, the printed team cheat sheet, the official stage results, and the race's @racetoalaska Instagram — the source establishing that a Paradox was entered in the 2026 R2AK as Jolyn Barley's Team Forget Me Knot, what the boat was rigged with, and what the race recorded as the entry's result.
* [Saanich News (Vancouver Island local press)](saanich-news.md) - A Black Press Media community newspaper on Vancouver Island, whose 5 June 2026 pre-race feature on the island's Race to Alaska entries is the only conventional press coverage found of the Paradox entry, placing it in its last week of preparation nine days before a start it did not make.
* [intheboatshed.net (Gavin Atkin's weblog)](intheboatshed.md) - A long-running UK small-boat weblog by boat-design writer Gavin Atkin, whose 2007-2014 posts are the archive's principal outside, non-participant coverage of the design — contemporaneous third-party attestation of Faith's 2008 Scilly cruise, five close-up photographs of Little Jim at Beale Park, and a comment thread in which two owners give the furling system's real-world line sizes and hand loads.
* [Catalyst — journal of the Amateur Yacht Research Society](ayrs-catalyst.md) - The AYRS's quarterly research journal, whose October 2008 issue (no. 32) carries Sven Yrvind's own contemporaneous print account of sailing Paradox and of the boat Matt Layden drew for him, illustrated with ten Layden drawings, alongside an editor's note placing the chine runner against earlier work by Walter Castles and on Wharram catamarans.
* [SAIL Magazine — "Go Small, Go Now" (David Bolduc)](sail-magazine.md) - Dave Bolduc's own 2014 first-person account of the couple's whole micro-cruising life — the February 1991 magazine photographs that led them to Matt Layden, the 1992 purchase and christening of Little Cruiser, seven winter Bahamas voyages year by year, Paradox's six-week Intracoastal Waterway delivery from Connecticut, and the 2013 solo Enigma cruise.
* [messing-about.com forums](messing-about-forum.md) - The American amateur-boatbuilding forum, home to the B&B Yacht Designs builder community, whose WaterTribe overlap makes it the archive's one outside-the-family record of Matt Layden's boats — a verbatim Layden race announcement, a professional designer's measured sail and capsize of an Enigma, and Paradox as seen by people choosing between designs. Distinct from the *Messing About In Boats* magazine above.
* [Small Craft Advisor (magazine and blog)](small-craft-advisor.md) - The American small-boat magazine that has covered this design and its designer more often than any other outside publication — the May 2007 Matt Layden interview that is his fullest first-person statement on chine runners, a 2009 Layden profile, the 2003 Cedar Key report covering two Paradoxes, and Sean Mulligan's own account of sailing "Scout" the length of Lake Havasu engine-less, the archive's only published magazine account of a Paradox written by the person sailing her.

* [Sailing and Paddling with Doug (Doug Cameron's blog)](capt-doug-blog.md) - The Blogger site of WaterTribe veteran Doug Cameron ("Ridgerunner"), who wrote the May/June 2009 Small Craft Advisor profile of Matt Layden and reprinted it in full — the archive's eyewitness source for Layden boats on the WaterTribe start line, and the only record of the finishing order of the 2010 Ultimate Florida Challenge.
* [70.8% (Thomas Armstrong's weblog)](70point8-blog.md) - An American small-craft weblog whose September 2008 "Microcruising" series set Paradox alongside Serge Testa, the Dyes, Webb Chiles and Charles Stock as one entry in a canon of small-boat voyaging — and whose pages carry a signed comment by Bill Serjeant giving the UK build order outright, Dave Bolduc's plans-ordering blurb quoted verbatim in public, and three dated 2008 photographs of "Faith" and "little Jim."
