# microcruising.com (David & Mindy Bolduc's website)

**microcruising.com** (`http://www.microcruising.com/`) is a personal "Micro Cruising" website tied to the distributor of the [Paradox building plans](/references/building-plans.md), the ownership and cruising of the Layden-designed [Little Cruiser](/matt-layden/little-cruiser.md), the restoration of [Swamp Thing](/matt-layden/swamp-thing.md), and the later purchase of Matt Layden's [Enigma](/matt-layden/enigma.md). It is distinct from the [Micro Cruising **Yahoo group**](/references/micro-cruising-group.md); this concept covers the **website** itself.

Unlike the [mailing-list archive](/references/mailing-list.md) (many builders' back-and-forth email), this is an **authored, first-person primary source** — an unusually authoritative one, given personal acquaintance with Matt Layden, firsthand examination of most of his boats, and three decades of firsthand micro-cruising experience behind it.[1] Where the two sources overlap they largely corroborate each other; where the website adds detail (exact design particulars, the full boat lineage with dates, the designer's stated design rationale, and long how-to articles), it is treated as high-quality primary evidence, while clearly-labeled opinions ("in our opinion…") are recorded as opinion rather than fact.

## Provenance and dating

The site is **effectively frozen**: its files were last modified around **December 2019** (the most recent content is a 2019 Cedar Key gathering notice and blog entries running to ~2018), and its newest dated article material runs through the early-to-mid 2010s.[2] It runs to **135 HTML pages** and **1,643 referenced images** (thumbnails, inline photos, design-particular diagrams, and full-size gallery photos). Because it is a static historical snapshot, its content is cited in the past tense and dated where the page gives a date, consistent with the bundle's historical-archive convention.

How the site looked from outside while it was still being added to is recorded once. In **September 2008** the American small-craft weblog [70.8%](/references/70point8-blog.md) called it **"the main portal to Laydens work,"** with "great links, cruises they've done, study plans, sketches, news and forums," and named its owners "the conduit for Matt['s] plans if you want to build a Paradox" — pointing readers at `microcruising.com/new1a.htm` for the current state of the [Swamp Thing](/matt-layden/swamp-thing.md) restoration. That is an unconnected third party's description of the site's function, and it matches the role the rest of this bundle assigns it. The same writer had mistyped the address as `microcrusing.com` two months earlier, a link that has never resolved — a caution for anyone following an outside citation of this site rather than the site itself.[3]

## Site structure (what lives where)

- **Boats** — Matt Layden's designs, each with a design-particulars box and history: "Matt's Boats" overview (`pictures.htm`), `paradox1.htm`, `swampthing1.htm`, `lc1.htm`, `other1.htm` (Terrapin & GJAC), `Enigma.htm`, `Elusion.htm`, plus study plans (`plans1.htm`), Don Elliott's sketches (`Sketch1.htm`), and tank-test models (`models.htm`).
- **Articles** (`Articlemain.htm`) — long-form pieces: the Small Craft Advisor interview (`sca1.htm`), "Cruising in Little Cruiser" (`article1.htm`), "Cheating Winter" (`article2.htm`), the "Microcruising Guide" (`mcg.htm`), "Secrets to Cruising" (`secrets.htm`), "Sculling With a Yuloh" (`WLL.htm`), a Cruising World piece (`cw1.htm`), book reviews (`reviewb.htm`, `sailing.htm`), and forum-question commentaries (`fc.htm`, `fq1.htm`, `fq2.htm`).
- **Projects** (`mainprojects.htm`) — how-to build/refit logs: hull repair, Little Cruiser refit, Swamp Thing rebuild, folding dinghy, cushions, painting, bow shed, sailmaking, and LED lighting.
- **Trips / Blog / News** — cruising reports and the news/blog archives (`list.htm`, `new1a.htm`, `new2a.htm`, `oldnews.htm`, trip pages, etc.), including the annual **Cedar Key** small-boat gathering.
- **Reviews / Links / misc** — recommended books and gear, "Famous Boats" (`famoussmallboats.htm`), external links, and a provisioning food list.

## Citing this source

Website pages are cited in the form:

> [microcruising.com](/references/microcruising-website.md) — "Page title," David & Mindy Bolduc — [detail].

The address is reconstructed from the identifier: `http://www.microcruising.com/PAGE.htm`.

Every page listed under "Site structure" above resolves at `http://www.microcruising.com/<PAGE>.htm` using the exact filename given there (e.g. `paradox1.htm`, `Enigma.htm`). Images referenced from those pages sit alongside them in the site's own directory layout — `Graphics/`, `images/`, and the per-gallery `Strefit3/`, `Sailconstruction/`, `NewCushions/`, `NewFloor/` folders.
