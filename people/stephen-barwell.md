# Stephen Barwell

**Stephen Barwell** is building the Paradox **"[SaSa](/boats/sasa.md)"** in Moreton Bay, Queensland, Australia, begun 9 June 2013, his first piece of woodwork since a school shoe rack made 47 years earlier.[1] Full build account: [boats/sasa.md](/boats/sasa.md).

## Boat

By early August 2013 he had shared photographs of the boat's progress, including a curved transom and a double-tiller self-steering arrangement, one tiller below deck for interior steering and a second above the aft deck.[1] Two and a half years on, with the hull roughly half built, he described himself as a "microcruising enthusiast" who "can even spell Sven Yrvind," and pointed to video of a Paradox self-righting in far-northern conditions as a convincing answer to a newcomer's worry about capsize.[2] SaSa's construction, her ballast, tiller system and finished mast are recorded in full on her own page.

## Hosting the Jollybod

Barwell also runs **"the Jollybod,"** a sailing, paddling and stealth-camping event held each winter and summer on North Stradbroke Island, open to any vessel able to start and finish in three feet of water, with a raft-up the day before the main event.[3]
