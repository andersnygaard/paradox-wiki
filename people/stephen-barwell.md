# Stephen Barwell

**Stephen Barwell** (Yahoo handle `surfgarden`) is building a Paradox, "**[SaSa](/boats/sasa.md)**," on North Stradbroke Island, Queensland, Australia, begun 9 June 2013 — his first piece of woodwork since a school shoe rack made 47 years earlier.[1] By early August 2013 he had shared photographs of the boat's progress, including a curved transom and a planned double-tiller self-steering arrangement.[1][2] Full build record: [SaSa](/boats/sasa.md).
