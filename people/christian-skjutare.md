# Christian Skjutare

**Christian Skjutare** is a boatbuilder from **Skåne**, in southern Sweden.[1] He began building the Paradox "[Martha](/boats/martha.md)" in August 2013, his first solo boat build after taking part in group kayak builds and completing one kayak entirely on his own, coming to the project after a 13-year break from sailing.[2] He gave her a first test on the water in July 2017 under a new name, "**Gripen**" ("the Griffon").[3] Full build account: [boats/martha.md](/boats/martha.md).

## Boat

Swedish plywood ran about a millimetre over the US-standard thicknesses the plans specify. Christian had already met the same mismatch on a kayak build and worked around it there by simply using the thicker sheet; he did the same on Martha.[4] Launched as Gripen, she handled surprisingly smoothly on that first outing, carrying two people well over 200 lb apiece under power alone, with no rudder or ballast yet fitted.[3] A house move afterward damaged her chine runners and part of her bottom, still unrepaired when Christian offered her free to other builders in 2020 rather than see her scrapped.[5] Full build record: [boats/martha.md](/boats/martha.md).

## Approach

He built at an unhurried pace: a year in, the work stood entirely to plan though most of it still lay ahead.[6] The same outlook carried into the boat's second life: he offered her free to other builders in 2020 rather than see her scrapped, judging small-river sailing away from the busiest waters well suited to a boat her size: "no-one respects such a small boat."[7]
