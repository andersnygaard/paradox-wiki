# Christian Skjutare

**Christian Skjutare** (Yahoo handle `c.skjutare`) introduced himself to the list in August 2013, announcing his intention to build a Paradox in Sweden, to be named "**[Martha](/boats/martha.md)**."[1] A first-time solo boat builder — he had previously taken part in group kayak builds and completed one kayak entirely on his own — he came to the project after a 13-year break from sailing he had long regretted, having done little sailing even before it.[1] Expecting most of his own questions on the list to arise from the language barrier rather than the boat itself, he asked whether any other Swedish Paradox builders were on the list.[1] His wife planned to help him set up a build blog.[2] A second prospective Swedish builder soon wrote in from Gothenburg, and Christian gave his own location as **Hörby**, in central **Skåne**, welcoming the news that more Swedes were taking up the design.[3] By August 2014, a year into the build, it was going entirely to plan but at a slow pace, with roughly 80-90% of the work still remaining.[4] Full build record: [Martha](/boats/martha.md).
