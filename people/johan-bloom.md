# Johan Bloom

**Johan Bloom** owns and sails **"Tetris,"** a [Paradox](/paradox.md) built in Divinycell foam-sandwich composite rather than plywood, out of the fjords of **Bohuslän** on Sweden's west coast. He has sailed her since 2020, by his own account the only Paradox then in Sweden or Scandinavia.[1] The hull is [Kjell Karlsson](/people/kjell-karlsson.md)'s "Paraplastic," built years earlier in [Sven Yrvind](/people/yrvind.md)'s "BRIS" foam-sandwich method, with a bronze-sheathed bottom and a carbon-fibre rig; full build account: [boats/bris-paradox.md](/boats/bris-paradox.md).

## Owning a hull he did not build

Bloom has said plainly that he did not build the boat himself: she was, in his own words, "built to Yrvind's ideas and standards of a light indestructable boat," fully insulated for winter sailing and suited to conditions on Sweden's west coast.[2] The hull still carries the nickname it was given when Karlsson built her, "Paraplastic," and the two builds were tied together in 2024, naming Karlsson as builder and Yrvind as his technical adviser.[3]

## Sailing the Bohuslän archipelago

Bloom sails Tetris year-round out of Bohuslän, the fjord-cut archipelago on Sweden's west coast, taking her out through the winter as well as the summer season.[4] Her shallow draft lets him reach ground closed to most sailboats: he explores the archipelago's shallow water and small islands, reachable, in his own words, "only to men in green boxes and kayaks."[5]
