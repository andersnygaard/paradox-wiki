# Steve Collins

**Steve Collins** is a Gulf Coast sailor who bought [Dave Skaife](/people/dave-skaife.md)'s Hawaii-built [Paradox](/paradox.md) in the spring of 2018, once it came up for sale in Pensacola, Florida, and brought her to a new home port on the Gulf coast under the name **"[Paw Paw](/boats/paw-paw.md)."**[1]

## Sourcing a sail (April-June 2018)

Shortly after the purchase, still unsure which sail had come with the boat — it looked stock — he asked the mailing list whether any company made sails for the Paradox.[1] The question went unanswered for nearly two months until a fellow owner, in the course of commissioning his own sail from a Rochester, NY loft, replied with that recommendation; full account at [boats/edge.md — the sail ordered, and a launch checklist (June 2018)](/boats/edge.md#the-sail-ordered-and-a-launch-checklist-june-2018).[2]

## Registering the boat (June 2018)

At the end of June 2018 he asked how to get his newly acquired Paradox added to the mailing list's own registry — the same week its longtime keeper, [OkieBob](/people/okiebob.md), had posted what he expected to be his last message handing routine registry upkeep to the group's moderators. Full record of the registry's own state at that point: [references/builders-and-owners-census-2014.md — a 2015 proposal for a group-maintained update, and a short-lived successor registry](/references/builders-and-owners-census-2014.md#a-2015-proposal-for-a-group-maintained-update-and-a-short-lived-successor-registry).[3]
