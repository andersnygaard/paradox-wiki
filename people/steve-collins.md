# Steve Collins

**Steve Collins** is a sailor on the **Gulf Coast** of the United States who became the second owner of [Dave Skaife](/people/dave-skaife.md)'s Hawaii-built [Paradox](/paradox.md). He bought her in the spring of 2018, once she came up for sale from Pensacola, Florida, brought her to a new home port on the Gulf coast and renamed her **"[Paw Paw](/boats/paw-paw.md)."**[1] He added her to the Paradox builders' owners registry that June.[2] Full build and ownership record: [boats/paw-paw.md](/boats/paw-paw.md).
