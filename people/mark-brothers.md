# Mark Brothers

**Mark Brothers** is a Paradox owner in **Newcastle, New South Wales, Australia**, who returned to sailing after a 20-year absence by buying and refitting a secondhand [Paradox](/paradox.md) in 2022, at the time one of only about four in the whole of Australia. The boat was [Nick Bell](/people/nickolas-bell.md)'s **"[Thor](/boats/thor.md)"**, built and sailed in South Australia; Brothers renamed her **"Pamela B"**[1] and has sailed her along the New South Wales coast, rivers and lakes since; in 2023 Michael Storer of Really Simple Sails drew the modern [Mk3 sail](/components/rig/mk3-sail.md) for her. Full build, ownership and cruising record: [boats/thor.md](/boats/thor.md).

## Finding a Paradox

He came across the design while looking for a boat small enough to launch and sail single-handed and to fit the tight gap beside his home, found that Paradox had no Australian kit supplier or builder and then found an old Paradox mailing-list post suggesting one of the country's boats might have been offered for sale the year before. A message to the apparent owner got a same-day yes, and since the boat was in South Australia, some 1520 km and a week's round trip away, the seller agreed to tow it partway to meet him at the New South Wales border at Mildura. The boat he collected was seven years old and in generally good condition, and he began refurbishing her that same weekend, aiming to have her ready by Easter.[2] A former competitive sailor whose racing career ran from dinghies to J24 keelboats, he has called the Paradox "the most fun boat he has owned" and one that can go almost anywhere, and counted four Paradoxes in Australia plus a single Enigma at the time, his own the only one in New South Wales.[3]

## Tuning by measurement

He answers claims about the design with measurement rather than argument. When the Paradox's windward ability drew criticism, he sailed a full circle without tacking around the Captain James Cook Memorial Fountain on Lake Burley Griffin in Canberra.[4] The same habit runs through his tuning: a lighter, larger sail, a halved water ballast and an electric outboard idled to windward for a free knot of speed all followed from working through the hull-speed formula rather than taking a claim on faith.[5] In April 2023 he fitted her with the [Mk3 sail](/components/rig/mk3-sail.md), drawn by Michael Storer of Really Simple Sails to lift her light-wind performance without changing her spars.[6] Full tuning and cruising record: [boats/thor.md](/boats/thor.md).

## Cruising Pamela B

Brothers records her voyages on his YouTube channel, [Cruising Pamela B](/references/cruising-pamela-b-videos.md), which has grown to twenty-two videos of coastal, river and lake cruising in New South Wales since December 2022, complementing the shorter updates he shares in the [Paradox Sailboat Facebook group](/references/paradox-facebook-group.md). Introducing the channel, he summed up what he had done with the boat: "lucky enough to acquire her and fit her out for luxury dinghy sailing."[1]
