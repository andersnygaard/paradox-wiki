# Jolyn Barley

**Jolyn Barley** is a solo sailor from **Comox, British Columbia**, on the east coast of Vancouver Island, and the first person on record to enter a [Paradox](/paradox.md) in the **[Race to Alaska](/merits/race-to-alaska.md)** — the roughly 750-mile, entirely engine-free race from Port Townsend, Washington to Ketchikan, Alaska.[1] Barley has entered that race twice under the team name **Forget Me Knot**, both times sailing alone: in **2024** on an 18 ft 6 in Hobie Tandem Island, and in **2026** on a Paradox, which at 13 ft 10 in was the smallest boat in that year's fleet.[1][3][5] That first attempt ended in withdrawal partway north after hull and ama leaks and other equipment failures aboard the Hobie; the retirement statement the race published on 19 June 2024 closed, "you can bet I'll be back to complete it one day … I will be just that much more prepared for next time!"[6] The Paradox entry two years later was that return.

## The 2026 Paradox entry

Barley returned in 2026 with a Paradox, registered on 9 April as Team Forget Me Knot — "Race vessel: Paradox / LOA: 13′ / Human propulsion: **Hobie Mirage Drive and Yuloh**" — and appeared on the race's printed team cheat sheet as boat **number 66**, a monohull of 13 ft 10 in from Comox, crewed by one.[1][3] The race's own fleet statistics, published on 13 April, made the point of it: "**Smallest boat: 13 feet. Team Forget Me Knot … sailing a Paradox. What's a Paradox? Google it,**" against a largest boat of 35 feet in a fleet of 16 trimarans, 37 monohulls, 2 catamarans, an outrigger sailing canoe, 4 kayaks, 4 rowboats, a paddleboard and a pedal craft, of whom 14 sailed solo.[2]

The declared propulsion is the unusual part. A Paradox carries a [yuloh](/components/yuloh.md) as standard, and the design's builders examined a **Hobie Mirage Drive** for it twice, in 2006 and again in 2008, and rejected it both times — the drive unit would block the flat cabin floor, its trunk would have to rise above the waterline and intrude through the sole, and a fin drive was judged ineffective on a hull as heavy as a Paradox (see [pedal power and why a Mirage Drive does not suit Paradox](/operations/auxiliary-outboard-power.md#pedal-power-and-why-a-mirage-drive-does-not-suit-paradox)). Barley's is the only Paradox on record **carrying both**, declared for the one race where engines are barred outright and a calm is settled by muscle.[1]

## The 2026 result

Barley was still preparing nine days before the start: local press on 5 June 2026 placed Forget me Knot, "from the Comox Valley," among three Vancouver Island teams "in their last week of preparation" for a race leaving Port Townsend on 14 June.[7] The race's official Stage One results then record the entry as **DNS — did not start** — the only such entry in that sheet, and the team appears nowhere in the Stage Two results.[4]

The race gives no reason. A Paradox was entered, registered, measured into the fleet and prepared for the hardest engine-free race in North America, and did not leave the line.[4]
