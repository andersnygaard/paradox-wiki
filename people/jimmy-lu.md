# Jimmy Lu

**Jimmy Lu**, posting as **`jimmylu88`**, built a [Paradox](/paradox.md) in **Taiwan** with no prior boatbuilding experience of his own, simply liking the design. Photos of his work-in-progress, posted to the group from July 2007 onward, showed rapid, professionally finished construction that drew unprompted praise from several established builders and moderators — and Bill Serjeant added him to the official UK-run owners/builders registry the same month he first appeared.[1] The boat launched **18 February 2008**, built in under eight months; her name, **"Auspice,"** does not appear anywhere in the record of the build itself, surfacing only six months later when another builder cited her as a fast-build example for a newcomer.[2][3] Full build account, the registry listing, and the launch record are on her own page: [Auspice](/boats/auspice.md).

Eight months after launch, still active, Jimmy Lu's own blog was found to be documenting the construction of a second, new yuloh — see [Auspice — a new yuloh, and the yuloh's roots in Taiwan](/boats/auspice.md#a-new-yuloh-and-the-yulohs-roots-in-taiwan) and [components/yuloh.md — the yuloh's living tradition](/components/yuloh.md#the-yulohs-living-tradition-and-a-taiwan-built-paradoxs-own-new-one).[4]

Later that year, confirmed a topping lift is not strictly necessary — Matt Layden's own design goes without one — but not something to want to be without, and was given an actual design to build from: a simple two-line, masthead-eye arrangement with photographs on file. See [components/rig/furling.md — the question recurs once more in 2009](/components/rig/furling.md#the-question-recurs-once-more-in-2009).[5][6]

Nearly a year later, having adopted that design on Auspice, he raised a new problem of his own — an outboard motor tangling with the mainsheet whenever he sailed — sparking a wider exchange that found no fix for his own boat, though a switch to a shorter-boomed junk sail was floated as one way around it. See [boats/auspice.md — a 2009 return](/boats/auspice.md#a-2009-return-adopting-the-topping-lift-and-an-unresolved-outboard-tangle) and [components/mainsheet-outboard-fouling.md — a 2009 recurrence across four boats](/components/mainsheet-outboard-fouling.md#a-2009-recurrence-across-four-boats-and-two-new-fix-ideas).[7][8]

In January 2010, weighing a cat-rig alternative for Auspice, he was pointed instead to the stock rig's own advantages, and separately settled on a ballast figure within the range other owners use after asking the list directly. See [Auspice — a 2010 cat-rig question, and settling on ballast](/boats/auspice.md#a-2010-cat-rig-question-and-settling-on-ballast) for the full exchange.[9][10]
