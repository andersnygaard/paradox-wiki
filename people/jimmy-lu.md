# Jimmy Lu

**Jimmy Lu** built the [Paradox](/paradox.md) "**[Auspice](/boats/auspice.md)**" in **Taipei, Taiwan**, his first boat, choosing the design simply because he liked it.[1] Photos of his progress from mid-2007 showed a rapid, professionally finished build, notable for a first-time boatbuilder.[2] She was built in under eight months and launched on **18 February 2008**; her name became known only six months later, when another builder cited her as an example of a fast build for a newcomer starting his own boat.[3][4] Full build account: [boats/auspice.md](/boats/auspice.md).

## Approach

He kept close to the stock design. Weighing a cat rig and a junk rig for Auspice in turn, he settled on keeping the plans' own lug sail once its advantages became clear.[5] He fixed her ballast the way other owners do, loading her to the plans' waterline marks with a helper watching from outside rather than guessing at a figure.[6] Some time after launch he built Auspice a second, new yuloh, documenting the work in two further entries on his own blog, in Chinese.[7]
