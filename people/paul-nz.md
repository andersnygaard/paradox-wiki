# Paul (New Zealand)

**Paul** ("Paul - NZ") is a prospective builder from the **south of New Zealand**. Two things drew him to the design in 2001: sailing from within the [enclosed cabin](/paradox.md) in his region's often rough weather and the boat's simple, robust rig, a contrast to earlier lake trips spoiled by fancy equipment failing in strong winds.[1] His questions were those of any new builder: where to get the [plans](/references/building-plans.md), the empty trailered weight and day-sailing suitability, handling in a 2 to 4 ft fresh-water chop and the [plywood count and thicknesses](/design/hull-scantlings.md).[1]
