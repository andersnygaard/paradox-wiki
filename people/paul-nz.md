# Paul (NZ)

**Paul** ("Paul - NZ") is a prospective builder in the **south of New Zealand** who joined the `paradoxbuilders` list in July 2001 — the design's first documented New Zealand interest.[1] Two reasons speak to the boat's appeal there: the ability to sail from within the [enclosed cabin](/paradox.md) in the region's often-rough weather, and the boat's simple, robust rig — a contrast to earlier lake trips spoiled by fancy equipment failing in strong winds.[1] His practical concerns were those of any new builder: where to get the [plans](/references/building-plans.md), the empty trailered weight and day-sailing suitability, handling in a 2–4 ft fresh-water chop, and the [plywood count and thicknesses](/design/hull-scantlings.md).[1]
