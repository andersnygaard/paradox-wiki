# Rick Langer

**Rick Langer**, an experienced boat builder by his own account from the northeastern United States,[1] sought the Paradox plans and building manual in 2009, intending to build a Paradox that winter.[2]

## Setting deck panels

Installing deck panels, he found that sheetrock screws driven first through a piece of scrap plywood worked well as temporary clamps until the epoxy set, the scrap enlarging the screw head's bearing area for a stronger hold without marring the surface; the resulting holes were afterward filled with whatever glue was left over from the next glue-up. Full account: [construction: deck and cabin beams](/construction/deck-and-cabin-beams.md#fastening-the-deck-panels-nailing-versus-screwing-and-a-temporary-screw-clamp-technique).[3]
