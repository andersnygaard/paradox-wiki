# Rick Langer

**Rick Langer** posted to the mailing list under the `farreach`/"sporadicfanatic" Yahoo account beginning May 2009, signing simply "Rick." An experienced boat builder by his own account, he first wrote in after seeing a reply to another correspondent's plans inquiry, asking for the same information and stating his intention to build a Paradox "the following winter."[1] No plans purchase, construction start, or boat name is ever confirmed on record; a severe wood-dust allergy, disclosed a decade later on the group's groups.io successor, curtailed his boat building altogether.[9]

## Seeking the plans and building manual

Having tried through the spring of 2009 to reach a source for Don Elliott's building manual with no success, he reported by late October giving up the attempt and deciding to go ahead and start his own project regardless, drawing on experience from having built other boats before; he still valued the manual as "an excellent starting point" and offered to pay full price plus shipping for a copy or a spare set.[2] No further report of the manual being obtained, or of construction beginning, is on record.

## A deck-panel clamping technique

Installing deck panels, he found sheetrock screws driven first through a piece of scrap plywood worked well as temporary clamps until the epoxy set — the scrap enlarging the screw head's bearing area for better holding power and avoiding marring the surface — with the resulting holes afterward filled with whatever glue was left over from the next glue-up. Full exchange: [construction: deck and cabin beams](/construction/deck-and-cabin-beams.md).[3]

## Recurring commentary

Asked for the YouTube link to a roller-reefing demonstration during the furling-system discussion, unanswered on record.[4] Questioned, after "[Zoë](/boats/zoe.md)" was reported destroyed by fire in transport, whether the wreckage really was the boat and whether the hauler could have staged the loss to avoid liability — a theory drawing a rebuttal from a fellow correspondent.[5] Weighing solar against wind and simply fitting an outboard for charging, judged a discussed "Supercharger" generator would suit his own RV's batteries, whose camper roof has room for more solar panels than a boat could carry.[6] Endorsed the proposed Penrose-triangle [sail insignia](/components/rig/sail.md) as "great" and "very fitting."[7] Recalling earlier interest in a Paradox cabin stove, shared a published ammo-can wood-stove design, prompting a chimney-placement technique discussion; full exchange: [cabin heating for cold-weather sailing](/operations/cabin-heating.md#a-2014-recurrence-an-ammo-can-stove-idea-and-a-flue-placement-technique).[8] Welcomed the group's proposed 2020 distance race as a great idea, and raised a rules critique of its "any body of water" format — full account at [merits/gatherings.md](/merits/gatherings.md#a-proposed-12-hour-distance-race-february-2020).[9]
