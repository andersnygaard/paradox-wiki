# Dave Fahy

**Dave Fahy** is a builder on the **Gold Coast, Queensland**, Australia, who built and launched an [Enigma](/matt-layden/enigma.md) 3.6, "**Goliath**," in March 2021 after an eighteen-month build.[1] Full build and sailing account: [matt-layden/enigma.md](/matt-layden/enigma.md#a-modern-built-example-goliath).

## Choosing plywood, then testing the ballast by sailing

Fahy built Goliath in plywood rather than the foam-core construction some other Enigma builders tried, a choice that came down to cost.[2] Once she was in the water he found the hull tender, and rather than accept the figure he started with, he progressively recast and added lead until she felt steady, nearly doubling her original ballast; the full weights and where they ended up are recorded on the design page.[3]

The same habit of correcting by sailing rather than by guesswork produced an early lesson. Running off downwind with too much sail up in a gusty channel, he let the sheet out too far and the boom blew forward around the mast, a stressful few minutes he fixed afterward by tying a stopper knot so it could not happen again.[4]
