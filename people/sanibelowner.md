# Andy

**Andy** built a Paradox, **"[Moxie](/boats/moxie.md)"**, through 2012, her hull at the sheer-clamp stage of assembly by February and her bottom's lay-up finished by August.[1][2][3] Full build account: [boats/moxie.md](/boats/moxie.md).

## Building to the plans' own thickness

The plywood's specified thickness, not a lighter substitute, was for Andy the boat's structural foundation: in his words, it is "what gives this little boat her character."[4] The fuller debate over trimming Paradox's panel weight is at [plywood grades and structural weight](/construction/plywood-grades-and-foam-flotation.md#a-2012-recurrence-the-9mm-substitution-question-restated-and-a-structural-weightballast-tradeoff).

## A sail of his own

In November 2013, a fellow builder's mainsheet-chafe fix on **"[Scout](/boats/scout.md)"** set his own plan for Moxie: a sheet hole drilled in the same spot and a sail sewn that winter with a window built in for visibility to leeward.[5]
