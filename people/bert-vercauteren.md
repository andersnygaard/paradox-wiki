# Bert Vercauteren

**Bert Vercauteren** is a boat designer in Belgium, active among Paradox builders from July 2001. He bought the [Paradox plans](/references/building-plans.md) not to build a Paradox but as design inspiration for a larger, two-person micro cruiser of his own, expecting it to differ from the Paradox in most particulars while remaining, in his own words, "the same in spirit."[1] He is known chiefly for an independent professional check on the design's stability.

## Checking the stability figures

A Paradox modelled in his own "Nautilus" boat-design software, built from the plans, reproduced Matt Layden's stability calculation in full, given a vertical centre of gravity as low as believed. Whether the design's flat bottom and side panels could take the full force of a breaking wave the way a rounder hull would not remained, for him, an open question drawn from a traditional European boatbuilding education. Both points are part of the design's stability record, at [self-righting and capsize recovery](/design/self-righting-and-capsize-recovery.md) and [blue-water suitability](/design/blue-water-suitability.md).[2]
