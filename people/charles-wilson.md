# Charles Wilson

**Charles Wilson** was a UK-based Paradox plans customer and early `paradoxbuilders` list member, active from October 2001, who bought a set of Paradox plans but ultimately built a different boat.[1][2]

## Contributions

- **Bought a set of plans**, reporting the ordering service as excellent — see [building plans](/references/building-plans.md).[1]
- **Raised the "stretch Paradox" question** — whether the boat could be stretched to 16 feet — which drew Matt Layden's rule of thumb for scaling scantlings by the enlargement factor. See [stretched Paradox](/design/ideas/stretched-paradox.md).[3][4]
- **Passed his spare plans CD** — which included the building sequence — on to a newcomer looking for a documented Paradox build sequence, having gone on to build a different boat himself. See [hull assembly sequence](/construction/hull-assembly-sequence.md).[2]
