# The Bolducs: David & Mindy Bolduc

**David and Mindy Bolduc** of Greensboro, North Carolina (Dave a carpenter, Mindy a pharmacist) have micro-cruised for roughly 30 years, with a string of two-up winter Bahamas crossings behind them. Dave distributes the [Paradox building plans](/references/building-plans.md) and founded the companion [Micro Cruising](/references/micro-cruising-group.md) group; together they own and cruise [Little Cruiser](/matt-layden/little-cruiser.md), a boat "very similar to Paradox," which anchors the couple's own site, [microcruising.com](/references/microcruising-website.md).

Dave met [Matt Layden](/matt-layden/biography.md) in 1992 and bought Little Cruiser that year; the couple went on to own three Layden designs, cruise the Bahamas in Matt's company for weeks at a time and discuss each of his boats with the designer at length.

The Bolduc record spans three pages:

* [Dave Bolduc](/people/bolduc/dave.md), the carpenter and plans distributor: his boats (Little Cruiser, Swamp Thing, Enigma), his community role and the wide body of design, construction and cruising knowledge he contributed, much of it relayed directly from Matt Layden.
* [Mindy Bolduc](/people/bolduc/mindy.md), pharmacist and co-skipper: she took the helm through a severe overnight gale, wrote the couple's own 2006 Outer Banks cruise report and has been filmed running Little Cruiser's sistership Enigma's roller furling gear by herself.
* [Matt Layden firsthand record](/people/bolduc/matt-layden-authority.md), the couple's in-person history with the designer: meeting him in 1992; owning and cruising his boats; the joint Bahamas cruises; and the techniques, figures and words of Matt's they carried back for other builders.
