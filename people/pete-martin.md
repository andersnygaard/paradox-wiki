# Pete Martin

**Pete Martin** (Yahoo handle `randoneur`, e-mail `pdmartin@…`, u=25214471) is a UK builder from **Cheltenham, England**, originally from Carrickfergus, Northern Ireland. He started construction of his Paradox on **1 December 2006**, launching her as **[JoHanna](/boats/johanna.md)** on the evening of **11 June 2011** after a near-five-year build.[1][2]

## JoHanna

The full build and launch narrative lives on **[boats/johanna.md](/boats/johanna.md)**; the hull was sheathed and turned on a home-built rolling frame through 2008–09, the rudder leaded and the decks laid, before a 2010 painting campaign repeatedly lost to weather pushed the launch past a hoped-for September 2010 date.[3] After launch, Pete moved his sailing writing to a separate "Paradoxically Speaking" blog.[2] He settled early on leaving out the plans' built-in water-ballast tanks in favor of a flexible bladder tank or old soft-drink bottles topped up with lead and stores, working out a periodic bleach flush to keep a bladder tank's water fresh between uses.[4][5] By 2013 JoHanna carried a De Graaff SB 730/2 ADJ road trailer fitted with flat rubber rollers, and an outboard cage to cut down mainsheet snagging, with the sheet leads later moved from the transom to the aft deck.[6][7]

- **Encouraging a struggling first-time sailor, and a furling-drum loading tip.** Answering a fellow owner's account of a difficult first sail — a topping-lift/sheet tangle while reefing — welcomed him to "the exclusive ranks of being a Paradox sailor," recalling his own similarly rough early outings (linking his own sea-trials report), and set out a standing furling routine: reduce friction everywhere in the system (halyard blocks, turnaround blocks, the furling axle), wear tough leather-palmed gloves against blisters, and load the furling drum completely with two full layers of line at the time of rigging, since a hard first reef under real tension pulls far more line off the drum than expected. See [furling system — drum purchase depends on how much line is already wound on](/components/rig/furling.md#drum-purchase-depends-on-how-much-line-is-already-wound-on-and-a-downhaul-vs-cleated-halyard-trap).[47]
