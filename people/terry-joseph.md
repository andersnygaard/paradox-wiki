# Terry Joseph

**Terry Joseph** (Yahoo handle `paradox_quixotic`) introduced himself to the mailing list in March 2015 as a brand-new Paradox builder, naming his boat **"[Quixotic](/boats/quixotic.md)"** from the outset. He had discovered the design and held the plans for just over a week before starting construction, working at a deliberately unhurried pace — "one a day" — while still enjoying the build and fascinated by the design.[1]

## Starting the build

Within his first week with the plans, Joseph had the bulkheads and transom done and had just started on the deck beams.[1] He planned to work through the mailing list's older digests for tips as he went.[1]

## Asking after "Small Fry"

The same introductory message asked the list for more detail on **[Geoff Davis](/boats/small-fry.md)**'s Western Australian Paradox "Small Fry," after coming across a secondhand account of a 2009 rolling and dismantling incident off Fremantle in near-hurricane-force winds.[1] Two days later he located and shared what is presumed to be the underlying news report; see [boats/small-fry.md — an unconfirmed 2009 report](/boats/small-fry.md#an-unconfirmed-2009-report-a-rolling-off-fremantle-and-a-presumed-news-source) for the full, still-unconfirmed record.[2]

## Building in Newcastle, New South Wales

A few weeks into construction, Joseph is based on Australia's east coast in **Newcastle, New South Wales**. Of the design he wrote: "The more I study Paradox, the more I think there's some magic involved. It's truly out of the box as far as solo longshore sail and oar stuff goes."[3]
