# Terry Joseph

**Terry Joseph** is a builder from **Newcastle, New South Wales**, on Australia's east coast. He discovered the Paradox and held the plans for just over a week before starting construction in March 2015, naming his boat **"[Quixotic](/boats/quixotic.md)"** from the outset.[1] Full build account: [boats/quixotic.md](/boats/quixotic.md).

## A deliberate pace

Joseph built at a deliberately unhurried pace of one task a day, enjoying the process throughout and fascinated by the design.[1] He found something close to magic in it, in his own words "truly out of the box as far as solo longshore sail and oar stuff goes."[2]
