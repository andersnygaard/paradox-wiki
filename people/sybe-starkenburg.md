# Sybe Starkenburg

**Sybe Starkenburg**, in **Montagu, South Africa**, began building an unnamed [Paradox](/paradox.md) in May 2017.[1] Lead for the ballast was scarce there, one of several materials harder to come by in South Africa than in Europe or the United States.[2] The hull was complete by October 2019, and the cabin, rig and windows followed over the next two years.[3][4]

## Getting the gudgeon right

Casting the rudder's gudgeon in epoxy took two tries. The first attempt, poured all at once into a single mould, heated up as it cured and bubbled its way out before it had set.[5] Smaller batches, thinner-spread or poured in stages with each one left to cool before the next, were the fix other builders converged on, and built that way the second gudgeon came out well.[6][7] He fastened the finished piece to the transom with both sealant on the bolts and epoxy bonding the gudgeon itself, reasoning that a bonded gudgeon could still be cut free later if it ever needed replacing.[8]

## A celestial navigation app

In December 2021 he released **StarFinder**, an Android app of his own for finding stars and taking a celestial line of position from the sun, moon and planets, work that draws on his background as a mariner and navigator. He sought beta testers from among fellow builders ahead of its release on Google Play.[9]
