# Mindy Bolduc

*Part of the [Bolduc](/people/bolduc.md) record; see also her cruising partner [Dave Bolduc](/people/bolduc/dave.md) and the couple's shared [Matt Layden firsthand record](/people/bolduc/matt-layden-authority.md).*

**Mindy Bolduc** is a pharmacist and the cruising partner of [Dave Bolduc](/people/bolduc/dave.md), distributor of the [Paradox building plans](/references/building-plans.md).[1] With Dave (a carpenter), she crewed roughly six two-up winter cruises to the Bahamas aboard [Little Cruiser](/matt-layden/little-cruiser.md), averaging about 800 miles sailed over 2.5-month cruises, a firsthand voice in the [microcruising.com](/references/microcruising-website.md) record of Layden-boat cruising.[2] She is a co-skipper rather than passenger: she held Little Cruiser through a severe overnight gale when Dave was too seasick to steer, wrote and signed the couple's own 2006 Outer Banks trip report and is on record running [Enigma](/matt-layden/enigma.md)'s roller furling gear by herself.

## At the helm, in her own hand

The Florida-to-Bahamas cruises are documented as a shared undertaking; the couple had made seven Gulf Stream crossings by the time of an early-2000s account.[2] The full cruise narrative lives on the boat page; see [Little Cruiser](/matt-layden/little-cruiser.md).

On the 75-nautical-mile Chub Cay-to-Bimini passage in 1993, a cold front built the wind from 15-20 kt to at least 40 kt overnight; Little Cruiser held hull speed (5 kt) downwind under a deep reef, surfing regularly in breaking seas. When Dave became too seasick to steer, Mindy took the helm and saw the boat through the remainder of the 17-hour passage herself, the clearest single instance in the family's record of her doing something no one else on the page did.[3]

Trim aboard Little Cruiser was always a shared, point-of-sail-dependent job, not Dave's alone: heavy gear was kept amidships at rest, shifted forward when beating and aft when running downwind in heavy air.[5]

She is also on camera running a boat's own gear by herself. Sailing [Enigma](/matt-layden/enigma.md) with Dave on Lake Townsend, near Greensboro (their local practice water, and their first sail aboard Enigma since Cedar Key), Mindy is filmed working the roller furling by hand, unprompted: "Mindy's actually doing it all herself."[12]

## Her own account

With no vacation time for the Bahamas that year, the couple cruised North Carolina's Outer Banks (Core, Pamlico, Croatan, Roanoke and Albemarle Sounds and the Neuse River) in Little Cruiser in 2006, following a big refit of the boat; the resulting "Cruising The OBX" report is signed "MINDY," confirming she authored it, and was independently corroborated by a third party's own account of the same cruise.[6][7]

She baked bread with [Matt Layden](/matt-layden/biography.md) too, on the 1994 joint cruise: at a Warderick Wells potluck, Mindy and Matt baked two loaves on Paradox's tiny kerosene stove, a scene told in fuller, Matt-facing detail at [the Bolducs' Matt Layden firsthand record](/people/bolduc/matt-layden-authority.md#cruising-the-bahamas-in-matts-company); what it says about her own hand in it is the baking itself.[4]

Interviewed on the Furled Sails podcast, she gives her own account of coming to sailing with no background at all. Raised in New York City and wanting to see something different, she came to Dave's leaky open Bluejay knowing nothing about boats: "I did not have any preconceived notions, so sleeping in an open wet boat when it rained... was fine by me."[11]

## Biography

A pharmacist by profession, Mindy had known Dave for 14 years as of a 2001 *Cruising World* profile.[1] She grew up in New York City. The couple met in September 1986 and married in 1989, consistent with the fourteen years of acquaintance the 2001 profile records.[11]

Her own career set the couple's calendar as much as Dave's did: a planned 2009 return to Cedar Key was called off after she injured her foot, and in 2019 Dave attended the gathering alone while she stayed home for work, evidence of a working life the couple's cruising fit around, not an absence needing explanation.[8][9] Her father, Alan Hart, died in October 2009 at 70; he had supported the couple's winter Bahamas cruises by "taking care of things back home."[9] Dave's seventh Bahamas trip, a six-week, engine-free, single-handed 2013 voyage aboard the smaller sharpie Enigma, was made without her; the prior six had been sailed together aboard Little Cruiser.[10]
