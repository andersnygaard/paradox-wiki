# Chuck Leinweber

**Chuck Leinweber** founded and publishes [Duckworks Magazine](/references/duckworks-magazine.md), an online magazine and plans outlet for small-boat builders. He had built his own Paradox, "Tennessee," by 2001.[1]

## Duckworks Magazine

Leinweber republished a [bottom-painting technique](/construction/bottom-painting.md#the-technique-bond-the-paint-to-green-epoxy) worked out among early Paradox builders, carrying it to Duckworks' own readership.[1][2]

Beyond his own magazine, he sells other designers' plans through Duckworks, among them Jim Michalak's and John Welsford's.[3] He has himself raced a Frolic2, a different small-boat design, in the WaterTribe Everglades Challenge, finishing in good shape.[3][4] Duckworks supplied materials as well as plans: one builder bought his fairing microballoons from "Chuck at Duckworks."[5]

## Tennessee

Topside paint on Tennessee came off in sheets within a week of application, after only a day or two immersed in salt water, an early lesson in matching a paint to its job.[1]
