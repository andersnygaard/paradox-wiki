# Cory Sellars

**Cory Sellars** is a carpenter in **Bruce County, Ontario, Canada**, finishing a part-built Paradox rescued from a burn pile after the death of her original builder, **Brian Prescott**. He renamed her **"[Prescott Eagle](/boats/prescott-eagle.md)"** in Prescott's memory and has been finishing her since 2019, aiming to cruise Ontario's Bruce Peninsula on the Great Lakes.[1][2] Full build account: [boats/prescott-eagle.md](/boats/prescott-eagle.md).

## A hull saved from a burn pile

Prescott had left the hull complete but for her epoxy sheathing and a few cut-outs, with the interior only just begun; it was not his first attempt at building a boat.[1] Sellars retrieved it from a burn pile and towed it home behind a trailer in a snowstorm, then kept faith with the original design: his own measurements found the hull tracked closely to the plans, with screws used in place of nails for the sides and bottom.[1] Naming her for the man who began her was his way of finishing somebody else's boat rather than starting his own from scratch.

## Approach

Sellars came to the Paradox as an already experienced dinghy sailor on the Great Lakes, mostly on Lake Huron and Georgian Bay, and is finishing Prescott Eagle with that cruise in mind rather than for day sailing close to home.[2] A carpenter by trade, he brought a CNC machine and a 2 ft x 3 ft duplicator to the finishing work, pairing digital fabrication with the boat's traditional plywood construction.[1]
