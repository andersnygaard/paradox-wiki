# Lezlie Henson

**Lezlie Henson** built the Paradox **"[Tardis](/boats/scout.md),"** a stock [Paradox](/paradox.md), in the Houston area of Texas, her first boat built from scratch. Construction began in 2010 and ran about two years, documented step by step on her own blog, [Lezlie's World](/references/lezlie-henson-blog.md).[1] She sold the boat almost finished in February 2013, without ever having sailed her; her successor, [Sean Mulligan](/people/sean-mulligan.md), launched and renamed her "[Scout](/boats/scout.md)," where the boat's full build, sailing and ownership record is kept.

## Building Tardis

She came to boatbuilding as a newcomer to the craft: she had learned to sail about three and a half years earlier on a 41 foot Irwin ketch, and built two plywood PDRacers first, to learn epoxy, fibreglass, rudders, leeboards and tools, before taking up the Paradox as her first coastal cruiser.[2] She cut the bulkheads and sides first, taking the advice to build a boat's smaller parts before its larger ones while the most room remains to work in.[3] Built to the stock plans plus [Don Elliott](/people/don-elliott.md)'s construction manual, she insulated the hull, built the plans' ballast and made her own gimbaled stove, all covered in full on [Scout](/boats/scout.md#the-build-a-stock-paradox-named-tardis). Painting the cabin's interior gave her pause: reluctant to cover the natural wood, she chose off-white in the end so the cabin would not be dark below.[4] She sewed the boat's mainsail herself, from a Sailrite kit.[5]

The build itself was a considerable undertaking for a first-time builder.[2] She kept the blog through it partly as a self-motivator: catching herself thinking a piece of work was "good enough," she would remember that other builders might be looking at her pictures, and "put a little more effort into making it better."[6] Lezlie's World ran from January 2011 to the boat's launch under new ownership in September 2013.

## Selling Tardis

She named the boat "Tardis," after the Doctor Who time machine noted for seeming bigger inside than out.[7] In February 2013, with the boat nearly complete, she sold "Tardis" to a buyer in Arizona, writing on her own blog that her divorce had ended the build, leaving the unfinished boat without its original purpose.[8] Mulligan bought and finished her, launching her that September as "Scout"; on her own blog she called "Tardis" "my baby that I created and nurtured," content that the boat had found a good home.[9] The sale and the boat's later ownership are covered in full on [Scout](/boats/scout.md#an-unfinished-tardis-and-a-uk-import-cost-estimate-january-2013).
