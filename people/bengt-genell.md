# Bengt Genell

**Bengt Genell** is a Paradox builder from **Västmanland**, in central Sweden, some two hours by car from the coast.[1][2] He came to the design through one of Yrvind's books,[3] and after two years of building launched his Paradox on **Lake Mälaren** in **July 2006**, naming her **"[Ladikan](/boats/ladikan.md)"** (Swedish for "the little box").[4] Full build account: [boats/ladikan.md](/boats/ladikan.md).

## Building on a tight budget

Bengt kept his build economical throughout: exterior grade plywood in place of marine grade, every outer surface fiberglassed regardless and ordinary screws where the specified fastenings could not be had.[2] He weighed cheaper polyester resin as a substitute for epoxy to hold costs down;[5] the fuller account of that question is at [construction/epoxy-coating-technique.md](/construction/epoxy-coating-technique.md#polyester-resin-versus-epoxy). He left out the built-in water tanks and the lightning protection, and built the cabin in two parts, with the aft section movable for easier boarding and exit. Rather than work out his own reefing and furling gaff, he copied the design from the Australian-built "[Zero](/boats/aussie-paradox.md)."[4] Plain, functional solutions took priority over fine finish throughout the build.

Ladikan reached the water in a windless spell on Lake Mälaren, leaving her sailing untested at first.[4] A registry of Paradox builders and owners, carried into a 2014 republication of data understood to mostly reflect the years around 2006 to 2008, records her with a modified cabin top and lists her as tested.[1]
