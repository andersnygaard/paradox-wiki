# People

The designer and notable builders/contributors in the [Paradox](/paradox.md)
community. Concepts are added here as they are sourced from the archive.

> The **designer, Matt Layden**, has his own top-level branch at
> [/matt-layden/index.md](/matt-layden/index.md) (his biography and his other
> designs). Individual built Paradox hulls live under
> [/boats/index.md](/boats/index.md).

# Concepts

* [Glen C. Maxwell](glen-maxwell.md) - A professional boatbuilder from Live Oak, Florida, who founded and stewarded the Paradox builders' mailing list, built and sailed his own Paradox "Zoë," sailed alongside designer Matt Layden, crossed the Atlantic aboard a larger Paradox-family sharpie, and in 2016 became the first American to take part in England's singlehanded Jester Azores Challenge under junk rig.
* [The Bolducs: David & Mindy Bolduc](bolduc.md) - David and Mindy Bolduc, North Carolina micro-cruisers, distributors of the Paradox plans, owners of Little Cruiser and the archive's deepest firsthand authorities on Matt Layden, whom they have known since 1992 and cruised the Bahamas alongside.
* [Sven Yrvind](yrvind.md) - Swedish designer and ocean voyager celebrated for extremely small blue-water boats (the "Bris" series), who never posted to the list but is one of its most-mentioned outside figures — the benchmark against which Paradox's offshore capability is weighed, and the chine runner's most authoritative external champion, a personal friend of Matt Layden who sailed Paradox himself. Sub-pages: [voyages and boats](yrvind/voyages-and-boats.md) and [Paradox and the chine runner](yrvind/paradox-and-the-chine-runner.md).
* [Don Elliott](don-elliott.md) - Early Paradox builder and author of the sixteen-part "Building the Paradox" construction guide, based in Tomah, Wisconsin, who built "The Edge" and advocated strict fidelity to Matt Layden's plans.
* [Alastair Law (Yeovil, England)](alastair-law.md) - A Yeovil, England CAD engineer who built and has sailed the Paradox "Little Jim" single-handed since 2003, the first Paradox built in the United Kingdom, testing where "sensible" ends and trusting the boat to bring him home.
* [Chuck Leinweber](chuck-leinweber.md) - Publisher of Duckworks Magazine and a boat builder active on the early paradoxbuilders list.
* [Chris Becker](chris-becker.md) - Australian (Brisbane, Queensland) Paradox builder who built his boat "Walden" from scratch, and an early canvasser of the community's who-has-a-Paradox roster.
* [Paul (New Zealand)](paul-nz.md) - A prospective builder in the south of New Zealand drawn to the Paradox for enclosed, simple-rig sailing in rough weather.
* [Bert Vercauteren](bert-vercauteren.md) - A Belgian boat designer who bought the Paradox plans as inspiration for a larger two-person micro cruiser, proposed a furling-system fix that field-testing later disproved, and independently confirmed Matt Layden's stability calculation using his own boat-design software.
* [Chris Curtis](chris-curtis.md) - Sandpoint, Idaho owner of the Paradox "[Scout](/boats/scout.md)" (ex-"Tardis"), her third owner — a lifelong shoal-draft sailor whose fleet has run through several Matt Layden and Layden-influenced boats.
* [Chris Morejohn](chris-morejohn.md) - A Florida Keys sharpie builder and liveaboard cruiser in the Layden/Bolduc circle, recorded at the helm of a Paradox making 6 kts in 25 kts on Exuma Sound.
* [Jeff ("Jeff UK")](jeff-doryman.md) - A UK builder from the Isle of Wight, active from 2001 to 2009 and one of the ten most frequent correspondents on record, who drew the from-scratch microcruiser "Paramour" and later built a lighter Paradox/Enigma hybrid, "Noli."
* [David Beard](david-beard.md) - A first-time Paradox builder from Tupelo, Mississippi, who bought and refitted a secondhand Paradox, naming her "Whisper," while also beginning his own Paradox from scratch, and became one of the archive's most prolific and enthusiastic contributors.
* [Charles Wilson](charles-wilson.md) - UK-based Paradox plans customer and early paradoxbuilders member who went on to build a different boat.
* [Paul V.](paul-v.md) - An American builder from the north woods who moderated the Paradox builders' correspondence alongside Dave Bolduc while building a Paradox to the plans, then designed a chine-runner sharpie of his own, "Whaleback," confirmed under construction in 2006.
* [Nels Arvidson](nels-arvidson.md) - A Canadian design correspondent from 2002 to 2017 who chronicled Matt Layden's 2003 Everglades Challenge win in real time, organized a volunteer translation of Sven Yrvind's book and sailed a Bolger Micro for over a decade while modifying a Jim Michalak Robbsboat.
* [Pete Hodges](pete-hodges.md) - A tall Florida paradoxbuilders member who planned and began a 10%-upsized "Paradox 1.1" and was a prolific source of construction, rig-scaling, and materials questions and ideas.
* [Jeff Forrest](jeff-forrest.md) - Southern Oregon builder constructing a Paradox from scratch, an early stretched-Paradox inquirer, and the list's main advocate of stitch-and-glue hull construction.
* ["guylita"](guylita.md) - A paradoxbuilders list member, known only by the screen name "guylita," whose questions on self-steering, lug-rig balance, and outboard ballast placement read as an experienced sailor's due diligence on the design.
* [Mat ("mat_man22")](mat-man22.md) - A paradoxbuilders list member and materials-minded contributor known for his metal-clad chine-runner shoe research and his scaling-formula guidance for enlarged Paradox variants.
* [Harald Lillevang](harald-lillevang.md) - A Danish list member and prospective Paradox builder, the first Scandinavia-based builder on record, drawn to the design after finding its sketches online in mid-2001.
* [David LeBlanc](david-leblanc.md) - A Seattle-area microcruising enthusiast who never built a Paradox but was a prolific contributor of materials, refit, antifouling, and small-boat knowledge to the paradoxbuilders community, 2002-2003.
* [William H. Longyard](william-longyard.md) - A Winston-Salem, North Carolina author who corresponded personally with Sven Yrvind, designed and built his own small boat "Lucky Town 7" and became a frequent technical voice on Paradox construction questions.
* [Ron Eike](ron-eike.md) - Non-building paradoxbuilders list member who served as the thread's small-craft sailmaker referral and its persistent WaterTribe Everglades Challenge commentator.
* [David C. Smith](david-c-smith.md) - A Pacific Northwest professional boatbuilder who built his own Paradox "Swash" and became a leading voice on design fidelity, sail trim, cabin heating, ground tackle, and navigation lights.
* [Andrew Graham](andrew-graham.md) - A Brisbane, Australia builder who launched his Paradox "Aussie Paradox" — named "Zero" — on 29 November 2003, and posted the archive's most detailed first-post construction account, including the plans-deviating choices that adapted the design to Australian plywood.
* [Kjell Karlsson](kjell-karlsson.md) - A Swedish builder whose Paradox "Paraplastic" is the only one on record built to Sven Yrvind's own "BRIS" composite method rather than plywood, and who later translated Yrvind's book chapter on Matt Layden into English.
* [Jack Gardiner](jack-gardiner.md) - A Rhode Island carpenter and tradesman who built the stitch-and-glue Paradox "Jill" and opened the archive's fullest debate on building the hull in steel instead of plywood.
* [Steve (Kansas City)](steve-kc.md) - A Kansas City, MO Paradox builder who began with the appendages in 2004, then sold his part-built hull's parts and supplies unfinished in 2011.
* [Derek Clark](derek-clark.md) - UK Paradox builder who built "Enuf," contributed a steel-hull mast query and mainsheet/outboard questions, and wrote a free community beginner's guide to building a Paradox; he sold her in 2006.
* [Bill Serjeant](bill-serjeant.md) - An Essex builder who ran the UK's principal Paradox web presence and registry from 2003 to 2009, built and cruised his own "Faith" and later took on a second Paradox, "Minnow."
* [James Hall](james-hall.md) - English Paradox owner who bought "Faith" from her builder, Bill Serjeant, around 2008, and cruised her out of Poole Harbour and the South West coast on long solo passages until his death in 2016.
* [Mark Waters](mark-waters.md) - A builder — Los Angeles, California in 2006, Portland, Oregon by 2008 — who completed his Paradox, "Raffles," strictly to plans, reaching 90% completion by February 2006, and whose finish was repeatedly praised as among the best built.
* [Matthew E. Noonan](matthew-noonan.md) - An Ithaca, New York builder and Cornell mathematician who started a from-scratch Paradox in June 2006 after building two Yost-designed folding kayaks and a PDRacer, kept a daily build log through his first week, reached the chine-runner stage that August, and then left no further record.
* [Stephen Yahn](stephen-yahn.md) - A Manchester, Washington builder on Puget Sound who built and sailed the Paradox "Ironsides" from 2006 to 2012, and was the community's second-most active correspondent of his own 2006-2009 span, after Alastair Law.
* [Bengt Genell](bengt-genell.md) - A central Sweden builder who spent two years building a budget, plywood Paradox, launched on Lake Mälaren in July 2006 as "Ladikan," the design's second independent documented Swedish build.
* [Sean Mulligan](sean-mulligan.md) - Owner of the Paradox "Scout" (ex-"Tardis," built by Lezlie Henson), based at Lake Havasu, Arizona, founder of the Havasu Pocket Cruiser's Convention, and a prolific source of Paradox sailing video. Not to be confused with [Sean Herron](/boats/defiant.md).
* [Pete Martin](pete-martin.md) - A Cheltenham, England builder, originally from Carrickfergus, Northern Ireland, who launched his Paradox "JoHanna" in June 2011 after a build of about four and a half years and has since sailed her on a 140-mile river cruise largely under yuloh power.
* [Ian Lang](ian-lang.md) - A North London, England builder who gathered plywood and timber quotes through spring 2007 and started construction on his Paradox, named "Irene May" after his late mother, in late June 2007.
* [Robert Biegler](robert-biegler.md) - A Norway-based owner who bought Derek Clark's former Paradox "Enuf," shipped her to the Trondheimsfjord area, re-fixed her ballast, and built two yulohs after the first was stolen.
* [James (Bradford, Yorkshire, UK)](james-bradford.md) - A Bradford, Yorkshire builder who began cutting plywood in January 2008 for a lightened, car-topping Enigma-style Paradox variant of his own design, after over a year floating and refining the same idea on the list.
* [John Gilbert ("JG")](john-gilbert.md) - A recurring non-builder technical commentator ("JG", gilberj55) active December 2006 - April 2008, contributing ballast physics, scaling, rig, yuloh, and construction technique across the archive without a Paradox of his own.
* [John H. Wright ("johninbastrop")](john-h-wright.md) - A Bastrop, Texas commentator active 2006-2010 and the archive's most persistent voice on Matt Layden's roller boom — which he built a version of himself — plus rig trim for windward sailing, the overballast-then-reduce launching method, and a firsthand dockside inspection of Elusion before the 2010 Ultimate Florida Challenge.
* [Dave Raftery](dave-raftery.md) - A Walpole, Massachusetts follower of Matt Layden's designs and author of the aiki.pbworks.com Paradox/Enigma wiki cited throughout this bundle — a Paradox plans owner whose own building intent settled on the Enigma 460, and the archive's most persistent advocate of that design as the alternative to enlarging Paradox.
* [Jim Baltaxe](jim-baltaxe.md) - An Island Bay, Wellington, New Zealand correspondent with a professional composite-construction background who weighed building a Paradox in foam/glass before ordering and receiving stock plans in early 2008.
* [Jimmy Lu](jimmy-lu.md) - A Taiwan-based first-time boatbuilder (jimmylu88) whose rapid, professionally finished Paradox build drew unprompted praise from established list members and launched 18 February 2008.
* [Roselt Croeser](roselt-croeser.md) - A Western Australian amateur boatbuilder who built the junk-rigged Paradox "Planktos" between 2013 and 2016 across four locations while owner-building a house, and presented the build and its Shark Bay shakedown cruise to the Amateur Boat Builders' Association in April 2021.
* [Dave Skaife](dave-skaife.md) - An American microcruiser who built a Paradox in Hawaii and sailed it engine-free for four seasons (2014–2018) — Hawaii, the Gulf ICW, the Bahamas twice, and Cuba — leaving the archive's longest continuous first-person Paradox cruising log after Bill's Log at daveskaife.wordpress.com.
* [Alberto Callejon](alberto-callejon.md) - A Rawson, Chubut builder who started "Cachivache," billed as the most southerly Paradox on record, in May 2008 after weighing the design's suitability for the open South Atlantic.
* [Rodney Cannon](rodney-cannon.md) - A Venice, California newcomer to both sailing and boatbuilding who ordered Paradox plans in June 2008 and began construction that August on a boat later identified, in a 2014 census, as "Wizard Spawn."
* [David Nemo](david-nemo.md) - A UK builder who built and completed the largest Paradox scale-up on record — a 28 ft x 8 ft live-aboard at double every stock dimension, foam-cored and framed in reclaimed church hardwood, reported crossing the English Channel and bound for the Mediterranean by December 2009.
* [Eerik Mee](eerik-mee.md) - Eerik Mee is a boatbuilder in Tartu, Estonia, who built and sailed "THU," a Paradox enlarged 20% and fitted with the only cambered junk sail built for the design, launched in 2017; he has kept helping other builders into 2025.
* [Lezlie Henson](lezlie-henson.md) - The Houston-area first-time builder of "Tardis," a stock Paradox she built to a high standard over roughly two years (2011–2012) and sold on almost finished; the boat became well-known under Sean Mulligan as "Scout." Documented on her blog, lezliesworld.blogspot.com.
* [Tim McKinney](tim-mckinney.md) - A Texas sailor who owns a Paradox built by Michael Beebe of Rockport, Texas, started the "Paradox Sailboat" Facebook group in February 2019, and gave the archive its first candid modern owner's account of the boat, including a 2019 Texas 200 attempt cut short by a snake bite.
* [André-François Bourbeau](andre-francois-bourbeau.md) - A retired Québec wilderness-survival professor and birchbark-canoe builder who owns two Paradoxes, designed the Gorfnik, scratch-built a twin-masted Enigma ketch of his own design in 2021 and has served for years as a bridge for francophone builders joining an English-language community.
* [Simas Butavičius](simas-butavicius.md) - A Lithuanian builder whose exterior-grade Baltic birch Paradox, thinned to 12 mm bottom / 9 mm sides / 6 mm deck to offset the wood's extra weight, has become the archive's reference scantling scheme for Baltic and Nordic builders.
* [Andreas Westerberg](andreas-westerberg.md) - A Swedish builder who 3D-modelled his entire Paradox from the plans' drawings and CNC-routed the bulkheads and parts — the archive's only recorded digital-first, CNC-driven build of the design.
* [Christian Blohm](christian-blohm.md) - A north-east Ohio builder who spent months gathering materials before his first cuts in February 2023, then documented a careful first-time build, named "Rebel," still under way in his garage.
* [Erik Blohm](erik-blohm.md) - A Lake Erie, Ohio sailor who proposed capsize-recovery modifications to the design in 2016 and began building his own Paradox in February 2023, opening the archive's fullest modern discussion of carbon-fibre and other alternative mast materials.
* [Dave Fahy](dave-fahy.md) - A Gold Coast, Queensland builder who built and launched the Enigma 3.6 "Goliath" in March 2021, then substantially increased her ballast to tame a tender hull — the archive's only documented Australian Enigma builder-sailor.
* [Mark Brothers](mark-brothers.md) - A former racing sailor in New South Wales who returned to sailing in 2022 by buying and refurbishing a secondhand Paradox — one of about four then in Australia, Nick Bell's former "Thor," renamed "Pamela B" — and became the reference owner for the modern Michael Storer / Really Simple Sails sail.
* [Johan Bloom](johan-bloom.md) - A Swedish owner who sails "Tetris," a Divinycell foam-cored Paradox nicknamed "Paraplastic," out of the Bohuslän fjords year-round — the boat Kjell Karlsson built with Sven Yrvind's help (documented as the "BRIS Paradox").
* [Greg Lockhart](greg-lockhart.md) - A builder who began construction of "Endeavour" in June 2011, the same week Glen Maxwell's "Zoë" was lost to fire, and built consciously in her memory.
* [Andres Espino](andres-espino.md) - A Glen-L sharpie owner and five-time boatbuilder who never built or owned a Paradox, but who drove the list's long-running debate on enlarging the design and supplied most of its comparative sharpie, rocker, and scaling knowledge between 2008 and 2011.
* [Edgardo Filon](edgardo-filon.md) - A Mar del Plata, Argentina builder who requested the plans by post in 2009 and launched his own Paradox, "Nebula," by October 2011 — the archive's second known Argentine builder after Alberto Callejon.
* [OkieBob](okiebob.md) - An Oklahoma City first-time builder who scaled his own Paradox to 85 percent and named her "Paradox 357"; who built and kept the community's own builders' registry for three years; and who later bought a second Paradox, "Oogway," from Michael Beebe.
* [Richard (Tarn-et-Garonne, France)](richard-tarn-et-garonne.md) - A French builder near Montauban who ran the archive's most rigorously controlled resorcinol-versus-epoxy scarf-joint strength test and glued his own spars and yuloh with resorcinol, then built two other small boats before returning to his Paradox, "Kolaska," completing the beams by August 2014.
* [Perry Phillips](perry-phillips.md) - A Monroeville, Alabama builder who spent two years asking Cedar Key, junk-rig, and rig-weight questions and, drawing on his own West Wight Potter's grounding experience, started a stock-design Paradox in May 2009 — no boat name yet.
* [John Lehman](john-lehman.md) - A Milwaukee, Wisconsin builder who began cutting bulkheads for a Paradox in 2008, and separately built, sailed, and sold an earlier stitch-and-glue Paradox in the UK roughly a decade before that — no boat name for either.
* [sumedho1000](sumedho1000.md) - A handle-only correspondent who started building a Paradox in April 2009 and had bulkheads made, hull panels cut, and the mast and rudder under way a month later — no boat name on record.
* [Michael Beebe](michael-beebe.md) - A Rockport, Texas builder who launched his first Paradox in February 2012, built and sold a second — named "Oogway" by its 2017 buyer OkieBob, and possibly the same hull Tim McKinney later owned — and became a recurring "Meanderings" columnist for Messing About In Boats.
* [sanibelowner](sanibelowner.md) - A handle-only correspondent who posted construction-photograph captions of his Paradox "Moxie" in February 2012 — the only record of the boat in the archive.
* [Gunther Battista](gunther-battista.md) - An Austria-based first-time sailor and builder ("batejarcat") who pre-named his 20%-enlarged Paradox "Manatee" in March 2013 and began her construction that May.
* [Christian Skjutare](christian-skjutare.md) - A Swedish first-time solo boat builder ("c.skjutare") who built the Paradox "Martha," launched as "Gripen" in 2017 and offered free to the group in 2020 after house-move damage.
* [Stephen Barwell](stephen-barwell.md) - A North Stradbroke Island, Queensland builder ("surfgarden") constructing the Paradox "SaSa," his first woodworking project since school, begun June 2013.
* [Anders Nygaard](anders-nygaard.md) - A furniture-maker in southern Norway building "Miramis," a 10% scaled-up Paradox — the archive's clearest example of 3D printing and digital fabrication folded into an otherwise traditional plywood build, and the compiler of the 2023 overview "A guide to the Paradox sailboat."
* [Tony Barlow](tony-barlow.md) - The builder of "Miko," launched at Phuket, Thailand in March 2024 — the archive's only tropical-Asian build, and the one builder driven off epoxy for good by an allergy contracted during the build.
* [Benjamin Bloss](benjamin-bloss.md) - A Puget Sound builder, north of Seattle, constructing the Paradox "Pathfinder" from 2021 — a former research-ship deck officer turned licensed captain, and the video-logging builder whose table of contents for Don Elliott's building manual became a community tool in its own right.
* [Kevin Williamson](kevin-williamson.md) - The Perth, Western Australia owner who bought Roselt Croeser's junk-rigged "Planktos" in 2023 and renamed her "Skye" — the archive's authority on making a junk rig practical on a trailered Paradox, through a folding mast and an 86 cm mast extension.
* [Alex McGavin](alex-mcgavin.md) - An experienced aluminium boatbuilder who welded the first metal Paradox on record, a 3 mm 5005-plate hull, with a companion 4 mm hull built alongside it for his friend Scotty.
* [Rob Kellock](rob-kellock.md) - A New Zealand builder-owner who built his Paradox over four years for NZD 6,500 all-in, has sailed her hard since, and gives an unusually exact, self-bounded account of what a Paradox does and does not do at hull speed and what a build actually costs once the small items are counted.
* [Tom Sailor](tom-sailor.md) - An Australian builder who returned to a long-shelved Paradox in 2020 and took her from stored bulkheads to a flipped hull in four months, reshaping the bow to a bluff profile and planning a junk rig.
* [Nickolas Bell](nickolas-bell.md) - An Australian who built a heavily laminated Paradox and sailed her for years before selling her on, then took a tiny home-built pontoon houseboat 1,550 km down the Murray; gave detailed firsthand accounts of fastening without metal, furling under way and how the boat handles driven hard downwind.
* [Darren (Victoria, BC)](darren-victoria.md) - A Victoria, BC sailing instructor nearing hull completion in September 2013, building a split lug sail plan drawn from an AYRS junk-rig design and planning a Vancouver Island circumnavigation the summer after launch — no boat name yet.
* [phil_e.stine](phil-e-stine.md) - A handle-only prospective builder who opened the "Mast is offset?" and cockpit-egress threads, organized the group's sail-insignia photo album, and proposed an Enigma-inspired mainsheet routing, all in December 2013 — no boat of his own yet.
* [newingtonnate](newingtonnate.md) - A handle-only British Columbia builder constructing a Paradox named "Bye Bye Love," notable for eliminating the vent box entirely for extra interior room.
* [Rick Langer](rick-langer.md) - An experienced boat builder (`farreach`/`sporadicfanatic`) who asked for the Paradox plans and building manual in 2009 intending to build that winter, and contributed a deck-panel clamping technique plus recurring commentary over several years, with no boat name or construction ever confirmed on record.
* [John Starkie](john-starkie.md) - A Norfolk, England sailor who corrected and explained rather than built, arguing navigation rules by rule number, reasoning chine-runner aerodynamics from first principles and catching errors in rope, sail and rigging terminology, active since 2013.
* [Terry Joseph](terry-joseph.md) - A newcomer who discovered the Paradox in early 2015 and began building "Quixotic" within a week of receiving the plans, working at a deliberately slow, one-task-a-day pace.
* [Brian ("Brian in PA")](brian-in-pa.md) - An American correspondent from 2013 to 2020 in Pennsylvania who weighed Paradox against several of Matt Layden's smaller designs, built a working copper-pipe cabin heater and began building a Gorfnik ParaDuck that its designer addressed as "Kinfrog," the second one built.
* [William Johnston](william-johnston.md) - A first-time sailor who worked through common first-rig sail-furling and topping-lift difficulties with the help of established owners in July 2015 — no boat name or location on record.
* [Jolyn Barley](jolyn-barley.md) - A solo sailor from Comox, British Columbia, and the first person on record to enter a Paradox in the Race to Alaska — twice an entrant under the team name Forget Me Knot, on a Hobie Tandem Island in 2024 and on a Paradox in 2026, the smallest boat registered in that year's fleet and the only Paradox on record declared with a pedal drive alongside its yuloh.
* [Doug Cameron](doug-cameron.md) - The Sewanee, Tennessee WaterTribe racer ("Ridgerunner") who wrote the Small Craft Advisor profile of Matt Layden — an eyewitness outsider whose blog records four Layden craft on one Everglades Challenge beach and supplies the finishing order of the 2010 Ultimate Florida Challenge.
* [Tom Sorensen](tom-sorensen.md) - A recurring correspondent who built Jim Michalak's "Robbsboat" and weighed an actual Paradox as his next build, contributing scattered practical advice on steam-bending, materials, ballast-tank plumbing, and a cambered junk rig from his own separate trimaran — no Paradox plans, wood, or boat name ever confirmed on record.
* [Jack Brubaker](jack-brubaker.md) - A Bloomington, Indiana correspondent who supplied technique commentary on rudder loads, cabin visibility, and rig choice from 2014, and by November 2016 was building his own 18 ft enlarged Paradox with a composting head and a combined foredeck hatch and anchor locker of his own design.
* [Sybe Starkenburg](sybe-starkenburg.md) - A Montagu, South Africa bookshop owner who began marking out his own Paradox's bulkhead and transom panels in 2017 — no boat name yet.
* [Frederic Tallman](frederic-tallman.md) - A Central New York sailor, who signs himself Deric, who held Paradox plans and Don Elliott's build manual for years before buying Don's own completed Paradox, "[The Edge](/boats/edge.md)," from the Elliott family in 2017 and who later built a wood-hulled Gorfnik of his own, "Cracker Jack."
* [Stéphane Cordier](stephane-cordier.md) - A Belgian professional woodworker who began building a Paradox in November 2017, drawn to the design by Lezlie Henson's "Tardis" build blog.
* [Jörgen Agardtson](joergen-agardtson.md) - A Swedish builder whose Paradox, "Rahänd," has been under slow but continuing construction since at least 2017, most recently reported in November 2022 with a two-layer plywood bottom.
* [Steve Collins](steve-collins.md) - A Gulf Coast sailor who bought Dave Skaife's Hawaii-built Paradox in spring 2018, renaming her "[Paw Paw](/boats/paw-paw.md)."
* [Cory Sellars](cory-sellars.md) - A Kincardine, Ontario carpenter finishing a Paradox rescued from a burn pile after her original builder's death, renamed "[Prescott Eagle](/boats/prescott-eagle.md)" in his memory.
* [Kreig (Missouri)](kreig-missouri.md) - A Blue Springs, Missouri builder who finished his own Paradox and sailed her for the first time in the fall of 2022, then opened the archive's dedicated thread on lifelines and going out on deck.
