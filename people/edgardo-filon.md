# Edgardo Filon

**Edgardo Filon** is a builder from **Mar del Plata, Argentina**, who requested the Paradox and Enigma plans and building manual by post in May 2009, after an unanswered e-mail attempt, already in touch at the time with [Alberto Callejon](/people/alberto-callejon.md), then building "[Cachivache](/boats/cachivache.md)" further south on Argentina's Patagonian coast.[1] His own Paradox, **"[Nebula](/boats/nebula.md)"**, was launched and photographed by October 2011; her full build record is on the boat page.[2]
