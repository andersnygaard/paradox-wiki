# Edgardo Filon

**Edgardo Filon** is a builder from **Mar del Plata, Argentina**, who first wrote to the list in May 2009, requesting the Paradox and/or Enigma plans and building manual by post after an unanswered e-mail attempt — already in contact at the time with [Alberto Callejon](/people/alberto-callejon.md), then building "[Cachivache](/boats/cachivache.md)" further south on Argentina's Patagonian coast.[1] His own Paradox, **"[Nebula](/boats/nebula.md),"** was launched and photographed by October 2011, making him the archive's second known Argentine builder.[2]
