# Stéphane Cordier

**Stéphane Cordier** is a Belgian professional woodworker who began building a [Paradox](/paradox.md) around November 2017, with the bulkheads finished or very nearly so within the first month, drawn to the design after following [Lezlie Henson](/people/lezlie-henson.md)'s build blog for her "[Tardis](/boats/scout.md)" from its earliest days.[1][2][3] He already owned a 9-metre stainless-steel cruising sailboat of the "Esquilo" type, built for long-distance voyaging and associated with the French navigator Nicole van de Kerkoven; he sailed her mainly in Scandinavian waters but had long wanted a much smaller boat to reach places she could not go.[2][4]

## A woodworker's build

Woodworking is his trade; he found the manual side of the build no trouble, but the stem and the chine runners gave him more difficulty than the rest.[3]

## Among French-speaking builders

Within weeks of starting, Cordier found two fellow French-speaking builders: Sylvain Caro, who was hoping to get a Paradox project of his own under way, and [André-François Bourbeau](/people/andre-francois-bourbeau.md), the design's veteran two-Paradox owner.[3][4][5]
