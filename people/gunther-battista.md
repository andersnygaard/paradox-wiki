# Gunther Battista

**Gunther Battista** (Yahoo handle `batejarcat`, signing "Günther") introduced himself to the list in March 2013 at 44 years old, from Austria, new to sailing but working toward a license, already holding the plans for a [Paradox](/paradox.md).[1] He pre-named his intended boat "**[Manatee](/boats/manatee.md)**," for the quiet, cozy character the name suggested to him, and settled on enlarging her by the sanctioned 20% for the extra stores and living space a genuinely extended cruise — envisioned eventually as far as the Mediterranean and Atlantic — would need.[1] Construction began that May, with the side panels cut and the bulkheads next within a week of the first materials arriving, photographs of the cut panels posted the same week.[2][3] Posting again about eight months later, in February 2014, he reported building almost entirely alone through a stretch of family stress, and the earlier epoxy allergy largely resolved; the full build record, including that update, is at [Manatee](/boats/manatee.md).[4] Nothing further from him appears on the mailing list after that; by April 2015 a fellow correspondent, reviewing old photographs, judged the hull very nearly complete and asked after him, having heard nothing since — full account at [Manatee — no further word since February 2014](/boats/manatee.md#no-further-word-since-february-2014).[5] Nearly three years after his last message, "Manatee" was recalled again by name in a February 2017 exchange about enlarged Paradoxes generally — a confirmation of which boat was meant, with still no further word of Battista himself; full account at [Manatee — a February 2017 recurrence](/boats/manatee.md#a-february-2017-recurrence).[6] He broke that silence himself two and a half months later, correcting the guess that he was German or South American — "I am an Austrian" — and confirming "Manatee is still in my possession," though little advanced since 2014: a divorce in the meantime, and four children to support, explain the slow pace, with a boat trailer planned for the following year and building to resume after.[7] Two and a half years later, in November 2019, he confirmed the build was continuing under the same "Paradox Max (Manatee)" name, with new pictures expected soon, adding two days later that he had not been able to work on the boat at all between 2014 and 2019 and that progress since had resumed slowly but steadily.[8][9] Over the following two days he posted a further set of pictures documenting the windows, toe rail, interior paint, and the start of the vent trunk, then the insulation and cover plates, floor plates prepared for paint, and the side fans still being worked out in his head — full build record at [Manatee](/boats/manatee.md#updates-since-2017).[10]
