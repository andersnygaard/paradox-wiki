# Gunther Battista

**Gunther Battista** (Yahoo handle `batejarcat`, signing "Günther") introduced himself to the list in March 2013 at 44 years old, from Austria, new to sailing but working toward a license, already holding the plans for a [Paradox](/paradox.md).[1] He pre-named his intended boat "**[Manatee](/boats/manatee.md)**," for the quiet, cozy character the name suggested to him, and settled on enlarging her by the sanctioned 20% for the extra stores and living space a genuinely extended cruise — envisioned eventually as far as the Mediterranean and Atlantic — would need.[1] Construction began that May, with the side panels cut and the bulkheads next within a week of the first materials arriving, photographs of the cut panels posted the same week.[2][3] Posting again about eight months later, in February 2014, he reported building almost entirely alone through a stretch of family stress, and the earlier epoxy allergy largely resolved; the full build record, including that update, is at [Manatee](/boats/manatee.md).[4]
