# Gunther Battista

**Gunther Battista** is a first-time builder and sailor from **Austria**, new to sailing when he began and working toward a license.[1] He began building "**[Manatee](/boats/manatee.md)**" in May 2013, a 20%-enlarged Paradox pre-named for the quiet, cozy character the name suggested to him, with an eventual cruise as far as the Mediterranean and the Atlantic in mind.[1] He chose the sanctioned 20% enlargement over the stock size for the extra stores and living space a genuinely extended stay aboard would need, widening the existing bays by a few centimetres apiece rather than adding new bulkheads.[2] Construction stood idle from 2014 to 2019, then resumed and has continued since. Full build account: [boats/manatee.md](/boats/manatee.md).

## Studying other builds before cutting

Before ordering materials, he studied other builders' documented hulls in detail, weighing their differences against his own plan for the enlargement.[3] He built mostly alone.

## Two stages of construction

Eight months into the build, in February 2014, the pace was slow but steady: "will go on, am an optimist."[4] A mistaken guess that had placed him in Germany or South America was set right three years later: "I am an Austrian." Manatee was still his, with a trailer planned before building could resume in earnest.[5] Work stopped entirely between 2014 and 2019; once it picked up again, it continued slowly but steadily, with new pictures of the windows, toe rail and interior to show for it.[6] Some of the boat's finer points were settled as he built rather than beforehand: the side fans' design, in his own words, "still works in my head."[7] Full build record: [boats/manatee.md](/boats/manatee.md).
