# Ian Lang

**Ian Lang** is a Paradox builder from **North London, England**. He started construction in late June 2007 on his Paradox, **"[Irene May](/boats/irene-may.md)"**, named after his mother, and built her to the stock plans with one departure, a window added to the cabin roof.[1] Full build account: [boats/irene-may.md](/boats/irene-may.md).

## Choosing materials with care

Before cutting any wood, Ian gathered UK plywood and timber quotes from three suppliers, inspecting an unusually cheap one in person to check its ply count and voids rather than trust it on price alone.[2] He settled on Robbins, the most expensive of the three but the one he judged most dependable in quality, and cleared over twenty years of accumulated contents from his garage into an 8 cubic-metre skip to make room for the build.[3]

## Building order

The standard advice given to new builders, to make the boat's smaller parts, the mast, rudder and yuloh, before finishing the hull, struck Ian as sound, but he judged himself one of those who needs a half-built hull in view to stay motivated to finish the smaller parts, and kept both moving together from a large insulated shed as well as his garage.[4] A 2014 worldwide census of Paradox builders still listed Irene May under construction, to the stock plans.[5] Full build order and progress: [boats/irene-may.md](/boats/irene-may.md#build-order-advice-fit-out-the-small-parts-before-finishing-the-hull).
