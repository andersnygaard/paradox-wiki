# Alex McGavin

**Alex McGavin** welded an aluminium [Paradox](/paradox.md) hull in **3 mm 5005-grade plate**, with a companion **4 mm hull** built alongside it for his friend **Scotty**. He came to the design as an experienced aluminium boatbuilder, having reviewed the available accounts online and finished an aluminium RIB before starting his Paradox.[1] Full build account: [Aluminium Paradox](/design/variants/aluminium-paradox.md).

## Alloy and gauge

His alloy and gauge choices rest on decades of RIB building rather than on this hull alone. He has built 4.2 m aluminium RIBs in 5005, 5052 and 5083, and one of his own 5005 hulls, built some 24 years earlier, remains sound after heavy use, which is why he accepted 5005's lower tensile strength for a boat this size.[2] Building both gauges side by side with Scotty, he found the thicker 4 mm plate easier to work and, cost aside, would choose it again over the cheaper 3 mm.[3][4] Full technical account: [Aluminium Paradox](/design/variants/aluminium-paradox.md).

## Progress

Work has gone in bursts around heavy work commitments. By December 2025 his own hull was close to being rigged, with Scotty's half done, and McGavin remains available to answer questions from anyone else considering the material.[4]
