# Alex McGavin

**Alex McGavin** is the builder of the **first metal [Paradox](/paradox.md) documented anywhere on record** — a hull **MIG-welded from 3 mm 5005-grade aluminium plate**, with a **companion 4 mm hull** built alongside it for his friend **Scotty**. The build itself, its method and its significance are at [aluminium Paradox](/design/variants/aluminium-paradox.md); this page is the builder behind it.

He came to the design already knowing the material. Before starting he had "looked at every video and photo on the internet that I can find," and was finishing an **aluminium RIB** when he joined the group in October 2024 and asked whether anyone had ever done a Paradox in aluminium.[1] Nobody had — the archive held only a week-long 2003 mailing-list argument about whether it could be done (see [aluminium and steel Paradox](/design/ideas/aluminum-construction-idea.md)) — so he built one.

## What his experience adds on alloy choice

McGavin's alloy judgment is the part of his contribution that generalises beyond his own boat, because it rests on decades of other work rather than on this build alone. He has built **4.2 m aluminium RIBs in 5005, and others in 5052 and 5083**, and reports that **one 5005 hull is 24 years old, has had "a very good work out," and is still going well** — his reason for accepting 5005's lower tensile strength on a boat this size.[2]

On **plate gauge** he is equally direct: having built both, "**I would do it in 4 mm** … the only thing is the 3 mm is a lot cheaper," and the thicker plate was **easier to build** with.[2][3] That is the archive's only first-hand data on how thin an aluminium Paradox skin can practically go, against the roughly 3/16 in (≈4.8 mm) the 2003 theorists had floated.

## Progress

Work has gone in bursts around heavy work commitments — a long pause, then a free weekend in March 2025 that also saw Scotty's 4 mm hull brought up to the same stage.[3] By **December 2025** the first hull was **close to being rigged** — "just about to put mast and sail on" — with the second **half done**, and McGavin offering to answer questions from anyone else considering the material.[2] His build is followed closely by **[Tony Barlow](/people/tony-barlow.md)**, who cannot use epoxy at all after developing an allergy building "[Miko](/boats/miko.md)" and would like to build a **20% larger Paradox in aluminium** next.[4]
