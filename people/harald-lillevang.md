# Harald Lillevang

**Harald Lillevang** is a prospective builder in **Denmark**, drawn to the [Paradox](/paradox.md) after finding sketches of it online in mid-2001; he decided to build his own, hoping one day to see a Paradox cruising Scandinavian waters.[1] He also tracked down sketches for "Big Cruiser," a Matt Layden concept, in the file section of [Micro Cruising](/references/micro-cruising-group.md); they turned out to be unfinished cartoons for a stretched Paradox that never reached final plans, as set out at [Matt Layden (biography)](/matt-layden/biography.md#asides-and-footnotes).[2]
