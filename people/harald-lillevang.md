# Harald Lillevang

**Harald Lillevang** is a Danish member of the `paradoxbuilders` community and a prospective Paradox builder. He found sketches of the Paradox online in mid-2001, could not stop thinking about the boat, and joined the group in early 2002 having decided to build his own, hoping one day to see a Paradox cruising Scandinavian waters.[1] He is the earliest Scandinavia-based prospective builder on record, extending the design's documented reach beyond its US, UK, Australian, and New Zealand builders (see [Alastair](/people/alastair-law.md), [Chris Becker](/people/chris-becker.md), [Paul (New Zealand)](/people/paul-nz.md)).

## Contributions

- Sought out **Don Elliott's "Building the Paradox" articles**, originally published in *Messing About In Boats* magazine, which surfaced their price and ordering details ($8 plus shipping) for the group — see [Don Elliott's "Building the Paradox" articles](/references/building-the-paradox-articles.md).[1]
- Located the "interesting sketches" in the `microcruising` Yahoo group's files section that answered a standing query about a Matt Layden design called "Big Cruiser" — confirmed ten months later as Matt's own unfinished cartoons for a stretched/enlarged Paradox that never reached final plans (see [Matt Layden (biography) — asides and footnotes](/matt-layden/biography.md#asides-and-footnotes)).[2]
