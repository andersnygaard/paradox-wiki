# Chris Becker

**Chris Becker** is a Paradox builder in **Brisbane, Queensland**, on Australia's east coast, who began building the boat **"[Walden](/boats/walden.md)"** from scratch in 2001.[1] He is not to be confused with [Chris Curtis](/people/chris-curtis.md), the later owner of a different Paradox, "Scout."

## Boat

Becker cut the first panels for Walden from 9 mm plywood in August 2001.[1] The build stalled more than once over the following year: work and a period away from building kept him off it through the autumn of 2001,[2] a three-week military exercise interrupted the panel-cutting he had just finished by New Year's Day 2002,[3] and a house move that took roughly seven months, including rebuilding his workshop underneath the new house, held the boat at bulkheads and scarfed panels into August 2002.[4] The full build account, including her plywood, layout and fit-out choices, is at [boats/walden.md](/boats/walden.md).

## Approach

He tested before committing: a 1:10 scale model of the boat's parts, built to check the fit before cutting the panels,[1] and, later, a cardboard mockup of the cabin, built at home to check the interior before building it in plywood.[5]
