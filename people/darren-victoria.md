# Darren (Victoria, BC)

**Darren** is a sailing instructor and Paradox builder in **Victoria, British Columbia**. By September 2013 his build was in its late structural stages; his one real departure from the plans was a split lug sail, drawn from Slieve McGalliard's AYRS design "Catalyst 37," chosen over the stock rig for better pointing and more drive, along with beefier scantlings and spars for extended offshore work; see [junk rig](/design/ideas/junk-rig.md#a-third-builder-underway-a-split-lug-from-ayrss-catalyst-37) for the choice set against the fleet's other rig experiments.[1]

By that same September 2013, the plan was to launch and test the boat's limits over one season before circumnavigating **Vancouver Island**, a roughly 1,000 nautical mile passage, the following summer, with any crew willing to join him offered sailing instruction along the way.[1] A year on he tested raising the whole hull for extra headroom, 50 mm throughout with no structural issues and a further 50 mm planned at the cabin top; full account at [crew size and fit](/operations/crew-size-and-fit.md#a-2014-exchange-the-boom-mast-and-hatch-consequences-of-raising-the-cabin).[2] In 2015 a local Victoria foundry fabricated the fork for his tack and furler assembly from its own copy of the drawing; full account at [the tack strop](/components/rig/tack-strop.md#a-2015-exchange-why-the-fork-helps-pivot-bolt-orientation-and-a-caution-against-over-modifying-it).[3]
