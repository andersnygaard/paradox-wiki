# Jörgen Agardtson

**Jörgen Agardtson** is a Swedish builder of **"[Rahänd](/boats/rahand.md)"**, a [Paradox](/paradox.md) under construction since at least 2017. His first mailing-list appearance, in February 2018, reports building the mast the previous summer — drawing the plans' mast measurements out himself, as several other builders have, to make sense of them. See [components/rig/mast.md — reading the mast drawing](/components/rig/mast.md#reading-the-mast-drawing-a-recurring-point-of-confusion) for the wider recurring difficulty this drawing has posed.[1] He resurfaced in November 2022, still at work on the boat — "a very slow one indeed" — and described his own bottom-panel construction: two layers of 12mm plywood in place of the plans' single 18mm sheet, with each layer's length-splice landing at bulkhead 3, illustrated with a photograph of the work.[2]
