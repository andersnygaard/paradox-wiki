# Jörgen Agardtson

**Jörgen Agardtson** is a Swedish builder of the Paradox **"[Rahänd](/boats/rahand.md)"**, which he has been building since 2017, when he made her mast himself.[1] Full build account: [boats/rahand.md](/boats/rahand.md).

## Boat

Rahänd's bottom panel departs from the plans' single 18mm sheet, built up instead from two layers of 12mm plywood with each layer's length splice landing at bulkhead 3.[2]

## Approach

He draws the plans' own measurements out by hand to work through them rather than trust the drawing alone, a step several other builders have also found necessary; see [components/rig/mast.md](/components/rig/mast.md#reading-the-mast-drawing-a-recurring-point-of-confusion) for the wider difficulty this drawing has posed.[1] The build continues at its own pace: by late 2022 it was, in his own words, "a very slow one indeed."[2]
