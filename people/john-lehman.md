# John Lehman

**John Lehman**, of **Milwaukee, Wisconsin**, sails mainly on Lake Michigan. He built a stitch-and-glue [Paradox](/paradox.md) in the United Kingdom, sailed her, sold her on returning to the United States and later began a second Paradox at home in Milwaukee.

## A stitch-and-glue Paradox in Cornwall

Built in the United Kingdom about a decade before 2011, this Paradox was built stitch-and-glue rather than with a chine log, plastic cable ties standing in for conventional stitching.[1] He sailed her single-handed around the Cornwall coast and sold her to a buyer on returning to the United States, calling her easy to launch, store and sail, very comfortable and "the best boat to sail I've ever had."[1] The full build sequence is at [construction/chine-log-vs-taped-seam.md](/construction/chine-log-vs-taped-seam.md#a-further-2011-exchange-whether-stitch-and-glue-changes-paradoxs-sailing-characteristics-and-two-further-builders-methods).

## A second Paradox in Milwaukee

By February 2008 he had begun cutting the bulkheads for a second Paradox, this one in Milwaukee.[2] Weighing bare wood, unthickened epoxy, varnish and paint for the interior, he settled on Cetol, following its own instructions to apply it over bare wood despite never having used it before.[3] The wider comparison of interior finishes for a Paradox is at [construction/epoxy-coating-technique.md](/construction/epoxy-coating-technique.md#interior-finish-bare-wood-epoxy-varnish-cetol-or-paint).
