# John Lehman

**John Lehman** (Yahoo handle `johnlehman3101`, signs "John Lehman") is a Milwaukee, Wisconsin builder who sails mainly on Lake Michigan. Two distinct Paradox construction efforts are on record under his name: a stitch-and-glue Paradox built in the UK roughly a decade before 2011, already completed, sailed, and sold by the time he described it; and a second Paradox begun in Milwaukee in February 2008. Neither carries a boat name.

## An earlier, UK-built Paradox — completed, sailed, and sold

Describing his own stitch-and-glue construction method in 2011, he gave one of the fuller first-person build accounts of a stitch-and-glue Paradox on record: side and bottom panels scarfed, temporary bracing to hold the correct hull shape, West System epoxy with its own brown thickener for the fillets, each fillet reinforced with two strips of 6 oz fiberglass tape. Bulkheads were cut from plywood to fit once the sides and bottom were already assembled, their corners notched to clear the fillets — the notches doubling as limber holes. Thin plastic cable ties served as the stitch fasteners: covered over by the fillet on the inside, trimmed flush and melted back into the plywood with a soldering-iron tip on the outside, the resulting hole filled with resin.[1]

He built this boat in the UK roughly ten years before that account, sailed her single-handed around the Cornwall coast, and sold her to a buyer on returning to the United States — "the best boat to sail I've ever had," easy to launch, store, and sail, and extremely comfortable.[1] Asked directly whether the boat was still in the UK, where in Cornwall he had sailed her, and for further stories, no answer is on record.[2]

## A second Paradox, begun in Milwaukee (2008)

In February 2008 he was "just starting to cut out the bulkheads" for a separate Paradox, asking the list's opinion on interior-finish options — bare wood, unthickened epoxy, varnish, or paint — while noting the plans require clear, unfinished areas left at the bulkheads for filleting them to the sides.[3] He settled on trying Cetol on at least some interior surfaces, having read that it should go over bare wood, despite never having used it before.[4]

## Two builds, not explicitly connected

The 2011 UK-boat account and the 2008 Milwaukee build do not square easily on the numbers alone — a boat "built... about ten years" before 2011 would date to roughly 2001, while the 2008 messages describe a Paradox only then having its bulkheads cut, in a different country. The two efforts are recorded here as they stand in the archive, genuinely distinct rather than merged, since no message on record explicitly connects — or separates — them further.
