# sumedho1000

**sumedho1000** posted to the mailing list under that Yahoo account alone — no distinct profile handle, no surname, and no location is ever given. He announced having "just started building a paradox" in April 2009, and a month later reported bulkheads made, hull panels cut, and the mast and rudder under way. No boat name is on record.

## Plan-reading questions at the start of the build

His first message raised three plan-reading questions at once: whether the side-panel scarf should be lengthened beyond the plans' own 110 mm to meet a general 12-to-1 rule of thumb, whether the vent trunk's drain runs all the way through the hull, and whether the bottom lightning ground can be glassed over or must stay in bare electrical contact with the water. All three drew answers from established builders — full exchanges at [scarf joints — a 2009 scarf-length question against the 12-to-1 rule of thumb](/construction/scarf-joints.md#a-2009-scarf-length-question-against-the-12-to-1-rule-of-thumb), [vent box — a 2009 drain-routing question, and a mast-step electrical-contact detail](/components/vent-box.md#a-2009-drain-routing-question-and-a-mast-step-electrical-contact-detail), and [lightning protection — where the copper flashing sits in the layup](/design/lightning-protection.md#where-the-copper-flashing-sits-in-the-layup-and-coppers-bonus-as-an-antifoulant).[1] He thanked the group once satisfied and went off to buy more plywood.[2]

## A month on: bulkheads made, hull panels cut

By May 2009 — bulkheads made, hull panels cut, and the mast and rudder under way — he proposed a hybrid joint for the already-cut hull panels: a plain butt join on the inside and a Payson-style taped joint on the outside, which would add roughly 110 mm to the boat's overall length. The answer favored trimming back to the plans' stock length instead, reusing the offcut as the joint's own backing block. Full exchange at [scarf joints — a further 2009 question: a hybrid inside-butt/outside-Payson joint](/construction/scarf-joints.md#a-further-2009-question-a-hybrid-inside-buttoutside-payson-joint-and-reusing-the-trim-as-a-backing-block).[3]
