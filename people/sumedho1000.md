# sumedho1000

**sumedho1000** began building a [Paradox](/paradox.md) in April 2009. Before cutting, three points in the plans were still open: whether the 110 mm side-panel scarf length was enough by a general rule of twelve times the panel's thickness, whether the vent trunk's drain ran all the way through the hull and whether the bottom lightning ground needed to stay in bare electrical contact with the water rather than sit under fiberglass. Each held as the plans already had it, and cutting followed.[1][2] Within a month the bulkheads were made, the hull panels cut and the mast and rudder under way.[3]

## A hybrid joint set aside

With the hull panels already cut, a hybrid joint was proposed for them: a plain butt join on the inside and a Payson-style taped joint on the outside, which would have added roughly 110 mm to the boat's overall length. The alternative taken instead kept to the plans' stock length, with the trimmed-off offcut reused as the joint's own backing block.[3]
