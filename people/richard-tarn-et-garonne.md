# Richard (Tarn-et-Garonne)

**Richard** is a Paradox builder in France's **Tarn-et-Garonne** department. In December 2010 he ran a rigorously controlled test of resorcinol against epoxy, then spent several years building two other small boats before returning to the Paradox; by August 2014 he had named her "**[Kolaska](/boats/kolaska.md)**" and was completing her beams, with the rudder next.[1][2] Full build account: [boats/kolaska.md](/boats/kolaska.md).

## Testing before building

A scarf-joint strength comparison, resorcinol against epoxy under lead-weighted load to failure, found resorcinol the more flexible adhesive under stress in the cold, at the cost of needing more clamping pressure to cure well; he went on to glue all his own spars and the yuloh with it. Full account: [boats/kolaska.md](/boats/kolaska.md#a-controlled-resorcinol-versus-epoxy-strength-test) and [construction/fastening-and-gluing.md](/construction/fastening-and-gluing.md#a-resorcinol-alternative-to-epoxy).[1] The same care in testing before committing shaped his trailer as well: its launch angle, bed length and recovery angle were worked out against an established Paradox's own trailer rather than to guesswork. Full account: [operations/trailering.md](/operations/trailering.md#a-swing-beam-roller-trailer-for-johanna-july-2011).[3]

## Returning to the Paradox

Between the glue test and the boat it served, he built two other small craft, a Selway-Fisher 50/50 sailing canoe and a Nick Schade Wood Duck kayak converted for sailing with an add-on rig by Mike Storer. By August 2014 he had returned to the Paradox, naming her "Kolaska" and completing her beams with the rudder next.[2] Full build account: [boats/kolaska.md](/boats/kolaska.md).
