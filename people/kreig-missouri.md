# Kreig (Missouri)

**Kreig** is a builder in **Blue Springs, Missouri**. He finished his own [Paradox](/paradox.md) and first sailed her in the fall of 2022, finding her reassuring under way.[1]

## Materials: Douglas fir throughout

He laminated the sheer and chine logs in two staggered-scarf pieces of Douglas fir, seeing no way to bend a single piece into the hull's curve; full detail at [laminated versus solid sheer and chine logs](/components/sheer-strakes.md#laminated-versus-solid-sheer-and-chine-logs).[2] The rest of the boat is built almost entirely of the same wood, including the deck beams, sourced from local lumber yards that had to special order the marine-grade Douglas fir plywood. He judges the finished hull "extremely strong."[3] She is painted white with a teal-and-navy stripe, her mast stepped.[3]

## Ballast, an outboard and tacking

Her ballast is full water ballast plus close to 400 lb of lead, with 32 lb of it forward of bulkhead 1. A Suzuki 2.5 outboard mounted on the port stern lets one fewer 32 lb block of lead be carried on that side. Four further blocks, two per side, sit forward of bulkhead 3, in a layout he judges balances the boat well.[4] She tacks without difficulty so long as some momentum is carried into the turn.[4] Going out on deck at all, including docking and loading onto the trailer, feels more exposed to him than sailing itself.[1] Full ballast detail: [design/weight-and-ballast.md](/design/weight-and-ballast.md#ballast-placement-outboard-vs-centered).
