# Paw Paw

**"Paw Paw"** is a [Paradox](/paradox.md) built from scratch in Hawaii by **[Dave Skaife](/people/dave-skaife.md)**, who sailed her engine-free over four seasons (2014-2018) across Hawaii, the Gulf Intracoastal Waterway, the Bahamas twice, and Cuba — carrying no settled name throughout that whole record beyond "my Paradox," and only "Viking funeral" on paperwork. The full build and cruising history lives on [Dave Skaife's own page](/people/dave-skaife.md#four-seasons-under-sail-and-oar).[1]

## Sold, and renamed under new ownership (March-April 2018)

Offered for sale from Pensacola, Florida as Dave Skaife moved on to overland travel in Southeast Asia, the boat sold in March 2018.[1] Her new owner, **[Steve Collins](/people/steve-collins.md)**, surfaced on the mailing list the following month, confirming she had moved to a new home port on the **Gulf coast** and signing off "Steve C (Paw Paw)" — giving the boat her present name.[2] Still unsure which sail had come with her, he asked whether any company made Paradox sails; the question went unanswered for nearly two months, until a fellow owner, mid-way through commissioning his own sail, answered in the same thread with a sailmaker recommendation. Full account: [boats/edge.md — the sail ordered, and a launch checklist (June 2018)](/boats/edge.md#the-sail-ordered-and-a-launch-checklist-june-2018).[2][3]

## Added to the registry (June 2018)

At the end of June 2018, with the boat's ownership now settled, Steve Collins asked the mailing list how to get her added to its own builders-and-owners registry. Full context on the registry's own state at that point: [references/builders-and-owners-census-2014.md](/references/builders-and-owners-census-2014.md#a-2015-proposal-for-a-group-maintained-update-and-a-short-lived-successor-registry).[4]
