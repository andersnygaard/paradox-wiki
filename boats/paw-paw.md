# Paw Paw

**"Paw Paw"** is a [Paradox](/paradox.md) built from scratch in Hawaii by **[Dave Skaife](/people/dave-skaife.md)**, who sailed her engine-free over four seasons (2014-2018) across Hawaii, the Gulf Intracoastal Waterway, the Bahamas twice, and Cuba — carrying no settled name throughout that whole record beyond "my Paradox," and only "Viking funeral" on paperwork. Her full build account is on [Dave Skaife's own page](/people/dave-skaife.md).[1]

## The build

She was built trailerable and with fishing in mind, a configuration settled on once Hawaii changed its mooring-permit fee structure; a cheap trailer and hardware were sourced early on eBay to spread the build's cost.[2] Her mainsheet uses a deck-entry point like the one built into "[Zoë](/boats/zoe.md)" to accommodate a wind vane, adopted after the plans' transom entry proved to chafe running downwind, and a wind scoop paired with a fan suited her to Hawaii's warm climate.[3] She was registered in Hawaii, her builder reasoning that establishing ownership there had been simplest for the paperwork trail since most states want residency. By 2017, after two Bahamas seasons, she carried an electronic autopilot, a personal locator beacon, a submersible handheld VHF, four anchors and two cabin fans, and he judged her "pretty much turn-key."[4]

## Sailing record

She was sailed and sculled without an engine through four seasons of cruising between 2014 and 2018, through locks and calms as well as open water.[5]

In 2014 she made a roughly 415-nautical-mile inter-island cruise of the main Hawaiian chain, from Hilo on the Big Island up through Lanai, Molokai and Oahu to Kauai, with two four-day unbroken passages; a reef strike was repaired underway, and a tiller-pilot proved her builder's most valuable single piece of gear. Full account: [merits/skaife-hawaii-cruise.md](/merits/skaife-hawaii-cruise.md).

In 2015 she was shipped from Hawaii to Texas and sailed on across the Gulf of Mexico's [Intracoastal Waterway](/merits/inland-waterway-and-canal-touring.md), starting from Port Isabel, through the Louisiana and Mississippi marshes, the Chandeleur and Ship Islands and Florida's Big Bend.[6]

She crossed to the Bahamas twice: a Pensacola shakedown and Gulf Stream crossing to Bimini and the Exumas in 2016, then George Town, the Jumentos and Ragged Island in 2017.

In 2017 a 17-hour passage from the southern Bahamas took her to Puerto de Vita on Cuba's north coast; she cruised west to Cayo Guillermo and inland to Morón, then made an engine-free Gulf Stream return to Florida. Full account: [merits/skaife-cuba-voyage.md](/merits/skaife-cuba-voyage.md).

## Sold, and renamed under new ownership (March-April 2018)

Offered for sale from Pensacola, Florida as Dave Skaife moved on to overland travel in Southeast Asia, the boat sold in March 2018.[1] Her new owner, **[Steve Collins](/people/steve-collins.md)**, surfaced on the mailing list the following month, confirming she had moved to a new home port on the **Gulf coast** and signing off "Steve C (Paw Paw)" — giving the boat her present name.[7] Still unsure which sail had come with her, he asked whether any company made Paradox sails; the question went unanswered for nearly two months, until a fellow owner, mid-way through commissioning his own sail, answered in the same thread with a sailmaker recommendation. Full account: [boats/edge.md — the sail ordered, and a launch checklist (June 2018)](/boats/edge.md#the-sail-ordered-and-a-launch-checklist-june-2018).[7][8]

## Added to the registry (June 2018)

At the end of June 2018, with the boat's ownership now settled, Steve Collins asked the mailing list how to get her added to its own builders-and-owners registry. Full context on the registry's own state at that point: [references/builders-and-owners-census-2014.md](/references/builders-and-owners-census-2014.md#a-2015-proposal-for-a-group-maintained-update-and-a-short-lived-successor-registry).[9]
