# Quixotic

**"Quixotic"** is **[Terry Joseph](/people/terry-joseph.md)**'s [Paradox](/paradox.md), begun in March 2015. Joseph introduced himself and the boat to the mailing list together, having discovered the design and held the plans for just over a week before starting construction — a newcomer's build from the very first message, rather than one that earned a name only after progress.[1]

## Early construction

At the time of his introduction, the bulkheads and transom were already complete and the deck beams under way, worked at a deliberately unhurried pace of "one a day." Joseph described himself as thoroughly enjoying the process and fascinated by the design, and planned to work through the mailing list's older digests for tips as the build continued.[1]
