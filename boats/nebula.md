# Nebula

**"Nebula"** is [Edgardo Filon](/people/edgardo-filon.md)'s Paradox, launched in Argentina and photographed by October 2011 — the archive's second known Argentine Paradox after Alberto Callejon's "[Cachivache](/boats/cachivache.md)," built further south on the Patagonian coast.

## Launch photographs, and a name learned only from a caption

A handful of launch photographs of Nebula were posted to [Pete Martin](/people/pete-martin.md)'s own build-photo website rather than to the mailing list directly, with the boat's name and builder given only in the caption; Pete relayed the news to the list with congratulations to Edgardo and praise for the paint scheme, promising no further detail of the build itself.[1] A Brazilian correspondent replied with brief congratulations of his own.[2] No mailing-list account from Edgardo himself, describing the build or the launch in his own words, is on record.
