# Bye Bye Love

**"Bye Bye Love"** is a [Paradox](/paradox.md) under construction in British Columbia, named in her builder's only message on record, posted under the handle **[newingtonnate](/people/newingtonnate.md)**.[1] No further build detail, launch report, or real name is on record.

## The vent box eliminated

Unlike the stock design, "Bye Bye Love" is being built with no [vent box](/components/vent-box.md) at all — removed entirely for the extra interior room it would otherwise take up.[1] A fellow correspondent, weighing the resulting loss of ventilation, suggested cutting the forward-compartment opening down flush with the cabin sole instead — the fix already used on **"[Little Jim](/boats/little-jim.md#the-forward-stateroom-access-cut-down-for-bike-stowage)"** to stow a folding bicycle — while warning that closing the hatch in bad weather without the vent box's airflow could fog the ports.[2]

## Completed, launched, and a bigger-boat alternative suggested (2014)

By March 2014 the boat had come together and was in the water. In place of the vent box she carries a mast box instead, with a roughly 15 sq in forward box of her own and a wood-stove pipe fabricated to run to starboard of the mast; a cutout was made for a head. All her fiberglass was sourced indirectly through Fiberlay in Seattle, at roughly $118 for 5 gallons. Small next to the alternatives, she was judged worth it for being simply in the water and sailing; a builder wanting something bigger was pointed instead toward a Jim Michalak-designed hull at 22 or 29 ft rather than an enlarged Paradox — "same damn thing." Photographs were promised to Dave [Bolduc].[3]
