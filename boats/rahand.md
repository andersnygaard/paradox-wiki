# Rahänd

**"Rahänd"** is a [Paradox](/paradox.md) under construction by **[Jörgen Agardtson](/people/joergen-agardtson.md)**. His first mailing-list message, in February 2018, reports the mast built in the summer of 2017, drawn up from the plans by Agardtson himself to work out its measurements.[1] A second message, in November 2022, reports the build still under way — "a very slow one indeed" — and describes the bottom panel as two layers of 12mm plywood rather than the plans' single 18mm sheet, with each layer's length-splice landing at bulkhead 3, illustrated with a photograph.[2] No launch report is on record.
