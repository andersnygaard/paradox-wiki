# Boats

Individual, known **Paradox** builds / hulls — specific instances of the
[Paradox](/paradox.md) design, as distinct from designer Matt Layden's other
designs (which live under [matt-layden](/matt-layden/index.md)). Each concept
cross-links its builder/owner under [people](/people/index.md). Concepts are
added here as they are sourced from the archive.

# Concepts

* [Original Paradox (Matt Layden's prototype)](original-paradox.md) - Matt Layden's original prototype Paradox — built in about four months and launched down a ravine to the Salmon River in 1993, cruised thousands of miles including a tandem Bahamas passage with the Bolducs' Little Cruiser, and won the ~300-mile 2003 WaterTribe Everglades Challenge outright, finishing at Key Largo well ahead of the entire paddled field.
* [Zoë (Glen C. Maxwell)](zoe.md) - Zoë is Glen C. Maxwell's professionally built Paradox, launched in late 2001 and cruised for years afterward, whose build hours, ballasting, interior fit-out, and Cumberland Sound sea trial supply some of the best-documented real-world figures for the design.
* [Walden (Chris Becker)](walden.md) - Chris Becker's Paradox "Walden", built in Brisbane, Australia, from 9 mm plywood starting in 2001 — the earliest Australian Paradox build reported to the list, though an earlier, 2000-built example later came to light (see [Aussie Paradox](aussie-paradox.md)).
* [The Edge (Don Elliott)](edge.md) - Don Elliott's heavily built Paradox "The Edge", one of the first two Paradoxes known to have sailed, with a distinctive lead-ballast installation technique.
* [Whisper (David Beard)](whisper.md) - David Beard's secondhand Paradox, bought from original builder Bob Archibald and refitted, renamed "Whisper," known for a hard-won lesson in tacking a loaded lug-rigged Paradox, a jet-ski collision that proved the hull, and a parallel from-scratch build.
* [Little Jim (Alastair Law)](little-jim.md) - Alastair Law's own Paradox, built in a Yeovil, England garage and launched in Poole Harbour in 2003, whose build cost, weigh-ins, launch trials, and cruise reports (including a 27-mile passage to Portland) form the fullest single UK builder's record.
* [Aussie Paradox (Andrew Graham), named "Zero"](aussie-paradox.md) - Andrew Graham's Brisbane-built Paradox, launched as "Zero" on 29 November 2003 — briefly credited as Australia's first Paradox to sail before an earlier, 2000-built secondhand example came to light — notable for its Australian-ply-sheet-optimized cutting layout, widened transom, omitted mid bins, and other deliberate plans deviations.
* [Petite Lady (Hugh Miller)](petite-lady.md) - Hugh Miller's New Zealand-built Paradox, completed in 2003 as the archive's first New Zealand build — round birdmouth spars aside, her only recorded deviations from the plans are curved fore-and-aft cabin ends and shorter windows, and a January 2007 haul-out after four unmaintained years afloat found her sound throughout.
* [The "BRIS Paradox" (Kjell Karlsson)](bris-paradox.md) - A Swedish two-person Paradox variant built in Sven Yrvind's "BRIS" foam-sandwich composite method rather than plywood — a beamier, taller hull with a stainless-steel-sheathed bottom for ballast and rock protection, no water tank, and a carbon-fibre rig, built with the blessing of both Sven Yrvind and Matt Layden, and the source of the only English translation of Yrvind's own book chapter on Paradox; now owned and sailed year-round on Sweden's west coast by Johan Bloom as "Tetris."
* [Jill (Jack Gardiner)](jill.md) - Jack Gardiner's Rhode Island-built Paradox, launched as "Jill" at Gaspee Point, Narragansett Bay on 11 September 2004 — a stitch-and-glue hull filmed extensively under sail and power, including two days in a steady 20-35 mph wind and a close-quarters test against passing powerboat wakes.
* [Enuf (Derek Clark)](enuf.md) - Derek Clark's UK-built Paradox — renamed "Enuf" from an earlier working name, "So-So," on his wife's suggestion — built with a fully worked-out ballast layout, a fabricated tack-strut/boom-axle pivot joint, and a three-tone green paint scheme with a yellow bottom; sold within a month of launch on health grounds, departing for a new owner in Norway.
* [Swash (David C. Smith)](swash.md) - David C. Smith's Paradox, built strictly to the plans from Joubert Okoume BS-1088 plywood and Honduras mahogany, moored downriver from Deer Island, Oregon on the Columbia River — briefly listed for sale during a divorce in July 2004 before David C. Smith chose to finish her himself.
* [Ladikan (Bengt Genell)](ladikan.md) - Bengt Genell's plywood-built Paradox, launched on Lake Mälaren in central Sweden in July 2006 after a two-year build, with a modified two-part cabin for easier boarding, no water tanks, and a scratch-built wood reefing/furling gaff copied from the Australian-built Paradox "Zero."
* [Raffles (Mark Waters)](raffles.md) - Mark Waters' Los Angeles-built Paradox, completed in 2006 strictly to the plans and later trailered to Portland, Oregon — one of the best-finished hulls in the fleet, with a mahogany interior, steel weight-lifting ballast in place of lead, and a rudder-head-mount self-steering wind vane that took two years to build.
* [Faith (Bill Serjeant)](faith.md) - Bill Serjeant's UK Paradox, built strictly to Matt Layden's original plans after a kit deal fell through — started August 2005, still under construction and unlaunched as of the record (expected summer 2007), like [Enuf](enuf.md) at its own pre-launch stage.
* [Defiant (Sean Herron)](defiant.md) - Sean Herron's Paradox, under construction as of December 2006 with slight modifications from Matt Layden's stock design.
* [JoHanna (Pete Martin)](johanna.md) - Pete Martin's UK Paradox, under construction in Cheltenham, England from 1 December 2006.
* [Irene May (Ian Lang)](irene-may.md) - Ian Lang's UK Paradox, under construction in North London from late June 2007 and named for his late mother.
* [Noli (Jeff, UK; ex-"Paranigma")](noli.md) - A lighter, shallower-draft Paradox/Enigma hybrid built on the Isle of Wight from 2005 onward — the same length as stock Paradox but roughly 500 lb lighter and drawing only 7 in, restated by her builder as "a Paradox-sized Enigma"; no confirmed launch report survives after an April 2008 update.
* [Ironsides (Stephen Yahn)](ironsides.md) - Stephen Yahn's Puget Sound-built Paradox "Ironsides," started July 2006 in Manchester, Washington — a steel-reinforced bulkhead 3 for a wider berth, a side-mounted outboard fabricated to spare his hands, a shop-built vent trunk drain, machined rudder gudgeons, and a proposed pendulum self-steering weight.
* [Auspice (Jimmy Lu)](auspice.md) - A Taiwan-built Paradox completed and launched 18 February 2008 by Jimmy Lu in under eight months, whose rapid, professionally finished mid-build photos drew unprompted praise from several established builders; named "Auspice," a name that surfaced on the list only six months after her launch.
* [Small Fry (Geoff Davis; later Gary Nicholls)](small-fry.md) - Geoff Davis's Western Australian Paradox, registered out of Fremantle and cruised north along the WA coast to Jurien Bay, the Abrolhos Islands and Shark Bay — the only documented Western Australian build, sold in July 2020 to Perth sailor Gary Nicholls; also the sole canonical page for Geoff Davis himself.
* [Scout (Sean Mulligan; ex-"Tardis," built by Lezlie Henson)](scout.md) - The Paradox built in Texas by Lezlie Henson as "Tardis," launched by Sean Mulligan as "Scout" on Lake Havasu, Arizona, sailed for years by Chris Curtis in North Idaho and the Puget Sound and in 2024 sold on to Lee of Maine — documenting a fast road-to-sailing routine, heavy-wind and 2014 Texas 200 sailing, a 2013 UK-import cost estimate, and the boat's full four-owner history.
* [Planktos (Roselt Croeser)](planktos.md) - Roselt Croeser's Western Australian Paradox, built 2013-2016 across four locations for about $2500 while he was simultaneously owner-building a house, and converted from the stock standing-lug rig to a junk rig — the archive's only junk-rig Paradox built by converting the stock lug, and the only one cruised, shaken down on a passage to Shark Bay.
* [Cachivache (Alberto Callejon)](cachivache.md) - Alberto Callejon's Paradox, begun May 2008 at Rawson, Chubut on Argentina's Patagonian coast and finished around 2011 with the help of master shipwright Néstor Siri — "el Paradox mas austral," the most southerly Paradox on record, sailed on the Río Chubut and the open Atlantic with her transom rudder slot closed against the South Atlantic.
* [Wizard Spawn (Rodney Cannon)](wizard-spawn.md) - Rodney Cannon's Paradox, under construction in Venice, California from August 2008 — her name unrecorded anywhere in the archive itself, and known only from a later, 2014 builders' census.
* [THU (Eerik Mee)](thu.md) - Eerik Mee's Estonia-built Paradox, launched 2017 — scaled up 20% on every plans dimension and carrying a purpose-designed cambered Chinese junk rig, making it the archive's only built-and-sailed example of both the larger-Paradox scale-up and a non-conversion junk rig; ~240 kg of cast-lead-and-water ballast, an electric outboard, and a full electrical system, day-sailed on an inland lake.
* [Odyssey (Alexander Fadeev)](odyssey.md) - Alexander Fadeev's Paradox, launched with a teak deck in May 2011 out of Barnegat Bay, New Jersey — a capsize test hanging a tester from the masthead heeled her only to about 45 degrees, well past a UK owner's own boat under the same method, before she navigated a tricky ocean-to-bay inlet against a wind-opposed tide.
* [Miramis (Anders Nygaard)](miramis.md) - Anders Nygaard's Paradox, under construction in southern Norway — a 10% scaled-up hull in exterior-grade Baltic birch to Simas Butavičius's lighter plywood scantlings, distinguished by extensive 3D-printed build aids and fittings, a home-sewn cambered sail, and a decade-long correspondence with Sven Yrvind, who first pointed him at the design.
* [Endeavour (Greg Lockhart)](endeavour.md) - Greg Lockhart's Paradox, construction begun June 2011 the same week Glen Maxwell's "Zoë" was destroyed in transit, and consciously built in her memory.
* [Nebula (Edgardo Filon)](nebula.md) - Edgardo Filon's Argentine Paradox, launched and photographed by October 2011 — the archive's second known Argentine Paradox after Alberto Callejon's "Cachivache."
* [Moxie (sanibelowner)](moxie.md) - A Paradox under construction as of February 2012, known only from her handle-only builder's own two photograph-caption messages, which place the hull at the sheer-clamp stage of assembly.
* [Manatee (Gunther Battista)](manatee.md) - Gunther Battista's 20%-enlarged Paradox, pre-named "Manatee" in March 2013 for the quiet, cozy character the name suggested, and put under construction in Austria that May.
* [Martha (Christian Skjutare)](martha.md) - Christian Skjutare's Paradox, announced in August 2013 as his first solo boatbuild in Sweden, launched in July 2017 as "Gripen," and offered free to the group in 2020 after a house move left her chine runners and bottom damaged and unrepaired.
* [SaSa (Stephen Barwell)](sasa.md) - Stephen Barwell's Paradox, under construction on North Stradbroke Island, Queensland since June 2013, with a curved transom and a planned double-tiller self-steering system.
* [Pathfinder (Benjamin Bloss)](pathfinder.md) - Benjamin Bloss's Paradox, under construction north of Seattle since December 2021 and still unfinished — built to plan in ABX marine plywood with laminated deck beams, laid out first in CAD, and documented in a continuing series of video build logs that record the hull-assembly stage as it happened.
* [Miko (Tony Barlow)](miko.md) - Tony Barlow's Paradox, built and launched at Phuket, Thailand in March 2024 — the archive's only tropical-Asian build, fitted for light airs and heat with twin outboards, a cut-down electric trolling motor on lithium, extra solar, and air conditioning.
* [Bye Bye Love (newingtonnate)](bye-bye-love.md) - A Paradox under construction in British Columbia as of December 2013, her handle-only builder's boat distinguished by eliminating the vent box entirely for extra interior room.
* [Kolaska (Richard)](kolaska.md) - Richard's Paradox, under construction near Montauban in France's Tarn-et-Garonne department, completing the beams with the rudder next as of August 2014, after a controlled resorcinol-versus-epoxy test and two other small-boat builds along the way.
* [Quixotic (Terry Joseph)](quixotic.md) - Terry Joseph's Paradox, begun in March 2015 within a week of receiving the plans — bulkheads and transom complete and the deck beams under way at a deliberately unhurried, one-task-a-day pace.
* [Thor (Nick Bell; later "Pamela B," Mark Brothers)](thor.md) - Nick Bell's South Australian Paradox, built through 2014-15 with a boat builder's professional help on a birdsmouth mast and launched on the Murray River in June 2015; sold in February 2022 to Mark Brothers of New South Wales, who renamed her "Pamela B" and made her the reference boat for the Mk3 sail.
* [Pequeno (Alan Ferris; later André-François Bourbeau)](pequeno.md) - A Paradox built by Alan Ferris in Meaford, Ontario, Canada, launched 2014 as "Al's Boat," later renamed "Pequeno" by André-François Bourbeau of Saguenay, Québec, his second Paradox alongside "Ironsides" (renamed "Iota").
* [Oogway (Michael Beebe; later OkieBob)](oogway.md) - Michael Beebe's second Paradox, built in Rockport, Texas to the stock cabin plan and modified with bulkhead hatch-seats, a removable coachroof, and an all-locker subfloor, sold in January 2017 to OkieBob, who towed her home to Oklahoma and named her after the wise old tortoise of the Kung Fu Panda films.
* [Rahänd (Jörgen Agardtson)](rahand.md) - A Paradox under slow, continuing construction by Jörgen Agardtson in Sweden since at least 2017, with a two-layer 12mm plywood bottom in place of the plans' single 18mm sheet.
* [Paw Paw (Dave Skaife; later Steve Collins)](paw-paw.md) - Dave Skaife's Hawaii-built Paradox, sailed engine-free across four seasons of Pacific, Gulf, Bahamas, and Cuba cruising, sold in March 2018 to Steve Collins of the Gulf Coast.
* [Prescott Eagle (Brian Prescott; continued by Cory Sellars)](prescott-eagle.md) - A Paradox begun by Brian Prescott in Kincardine, Ontario, rescued from a burn pile after his death by Cory Sellars, who is finishing her toward a planned Bruce Peninsula touring trip on the Great Lakes.
