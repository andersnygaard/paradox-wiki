# Pequeno

**"Pequeno"** is a [Paradox](/paradox.md) built by **Alan Ferris** in **Meaford, Ontario, Canada**, and launched in 2014 under the name **"Al's Boat."**[1][2] She later passed to **[André-François Bourbeau](/people/andre-francois-bourbeau.md)** of Saguenay, Québec, who renamed her Pequeno and keeps her as his second Paradox alongside **[Ironsides](/boats/ironsides.md)** (renamed **Iota**) — lending her out to sailing friends so he always has company on the water.[1][3]
