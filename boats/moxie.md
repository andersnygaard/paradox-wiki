# Moxie

**"Moxie"** is a [Paradox](/paradox.md) under construction through 2012, named in the subject line of her builder's first messages to the mailing list — "MSV Moxie."[1] Her builder, posting only under the handle **[sanibelowner](/people/sanibelowner.md)**, gave no real name, location, or further build detail; the archive preserves his captions and material lists, not the images themselves. One February caption correction places the hull at the **sheer-clamp** stage of assembly at the time.[2]

## The bottom, finished (August 2012)

By August the bottom was complete, laid up in **3/4 in high-grade plywood** ("consolation for marine grade") under **three layers of 24 oz woven roving**, set in over **2 gallons of epoxy** across three coats (one mixed with 407 filler), then **3 1/2 coats of grey undercoat** and **two coats of black bottom paint** — captioned "Moxy's Big Beautiful Bottom."[3] No further message from this builder, and no launch report, is on record.
